Scale drawings and maps

A scale tells you how a length on a drawing or map compares with the real length.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A scale like 1 cm : 20 km means 1 cm on the map stands for 20 km in real life.

  • Map to real: multiply. 4.5 cm × 20 = 90 km.
  • Real to map: divide. 150 km ÷ 20 = 7.5 cm.

Ratio scales

A scale of 1 : 50 000 means 1 unit on the map is 50 000 of the same units in real life. 3 cm on the map = 150 000 cm = 1.5 km.

Scale factor in drawings

A floor plan with scale 1 : 100 shows a 4 m room as 4 cm. A model car at 1 : 24 is 24 times smaller than the real car.

Units: 1 km = 1000 m = 100 000 cm. Convert before you compare.

See three worked examples

Scale 1 cm : 25 km. Map distance 6.4 cm. Real distance?
  1. 6.4 × 25.

Answer: 160 km

Scale 1 cm : 5 m. A wall is 12 m. How long on the plan?
  1. 12 ÷ 5.

Answer: 2.4 cm

Scale 1 : 50 000. Map distance 8 cm. Real distance in km?
  1. 8 × 50 000 = 400 000 cm.
  2. 400 000 cm = 4 km.

Answer: 4 km
