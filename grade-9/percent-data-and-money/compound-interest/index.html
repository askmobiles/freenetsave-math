Simple and compound interest

With compound interest you earn interest on your interest, so savings grow faster each year.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Simple interest is paid only on the original amount (the principal): I = Prt. $1000 at 5% for 3 years earns 1000 × 0.05 × 3 = $150.

Compound interest is added to the balance, and next year's interest is calculated on the new, larger balance.

YearSimple (5%)Compound (5%)
0$1000$1000.00
1$1050$1050.00
2$1100$1102.50
3$1150$1157.63

Compound formula: A = P(1 + r)n, where r is the yearly rate as a decimal and n the number of years.

Debt works the same way: unpaid credit card balances compound too, often at about 20% a year, so they grow quickly.

See three worked examples

$500 at 10% compounded yearly for 2 years
  1. Year 1: 500 × 1.10 = 550.
  2. Year 2: 550 × 1.10.

Answer: $605

$2000 at 4% simple interest for 3 years
  1. I = 2000 × 0.04 × 3 = 240.
  2. 2000 + 240.

Answer: $2240

$1000 at 6% compounded yearly for 3 years
  1. 1000 × 1.06³ = 1000 × 1.191016.

Answer: $1191.02
