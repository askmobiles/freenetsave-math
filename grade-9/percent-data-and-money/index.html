Grade 9 Math: Percent, data & money

Percent change, proportions, scale, rates, averages, probability and personal finance. Ten lessons with examples, practice, a game, a test and challenges.

  1. 1Percent increase and decreaseTo increase by a percent, multiply by (1 + rate). To decrease, multiply by (1 − rate).
  2. 2Finding the whole from a percentIf you know a part and its percent, divide the part by the percent (as a decimal) to find the whole.
  3. 3Solving proportionsA proportion says two ratios are equal. Solve it by scaling or by cross-multiplying.
  4. 4Scale drawings and mapsA scale tells you how a length on a drawing or map compares with the real length.
  5. 5Converting rates: m/s and km/hTo change m/s to km/h multiply by 3.6. To change km/h to m/s divide by 3.6.
  6. 6Mean and the missing valueIf you know the mean and how many values there are, you know the total. Subtract the values you have to find the missing one.
  7. 7ProbabilityProbability = number of outcomes you want ÷ total number of equally likely outcomes.
  8. 8Earning money: gross payGross pay is what you earn before deductions: hourly wage × hours worked.
  9. 9Appreciation and depreciationItems like cars lose value over time (depreciation). Items like houses often gain value (appreciation).
  10. 10Simple and compound interestWith compound interest you earn interest on your interest, so savings grow faster each year.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
