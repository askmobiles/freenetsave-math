Earning money: gross pay

Gross pay is what you earn before deductions: hourly wage × hours worked.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Gross pay = hourly rate × hours. Ontario's general minimum wage is set each October; check the current rate before you budget.

Overtime

In Ontario, most workers earn overtime after 44 hours in a week, at 1.5 times the regular rate ("time and a half").

Example: $20/h for 48 hours = 44 × 20 + 4 × 30 = 880 + 120 = $1000.

Net pay

Net pay (take-home) is gross pay minus deductions: income tax, CPP (Canada Pension Plan) and EI (Employment Insurance).

  • Salary: a fixed amount per year, like $52 000 ÷ 52 = $1000 per week.
  • Commission: a percent of sales.
  • Piecework: paid per item made.

Your pay stub shows gross pay, each deduction and net pay. Always check the hours are right.

See three worked examples

$17.60/h for 25 hours
  1. 17.60 × 25.

Answer: $440

$22/h for 50 hours (overtime after 44 at 1.5×)
  1. 44 × 22 = 968.
  2. Overtime rate 33; 6 × 33 = 198.
  3. 968 + 198.

Answer: $1166

Gross $800, deductions $164. Net?
  1. 800 − 164.

Answer: $636
