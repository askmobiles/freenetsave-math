Percent increase and decrease

To increase by a percent, multiply by (1 + rate). To decrease, multiply by (1 − rate).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two ways to find a new amount after a percent change:

  1. Find the change, then add or subtract. 20% of $80 is $16, so an increase gives $96 and a decrease gives $64.
  2. Use a multiplier (faster): increase by 20% → × 1.20; decrease by 20% → × 0.80.

Percent change

Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100%. From $50 to $60: 10 ÷ 50 = 20% increase.

Careful: up 10% then down 10% does not return to the start. $100 → $110 → $99.

See three worked examples

$240 increased by 15%
  1. 240 × 1.15.

Answer: $276

A $65 jacket is 30% off. Sale price?
  1. 65 × 0.70.

Answer: $45.50

A town grew from 12 000 to 13 500 people. Percent increase?
  1. Change: 1500.
  2. 1500 ÷ 12 000 = 0.125.

Answer: 12.5%
