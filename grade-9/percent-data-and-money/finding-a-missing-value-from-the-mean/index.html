Mean and the missing value

If you know the mean and how many values there are, you know the total. Subtract the values you have to find the missing one.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The mean is the total divided by how many values: mean = sum ÷ n. So sum = mean × n.

Finding a missing value

Four test marks have a mean of 75. Three of them are 70, 82 and 68. The total must be 4 × 75 = 300. The three add to 220, so the fourth is 80.

Mean, median and mode

  • Mean: the average (total ÷ count).
  • Median: the middle value when sorted.
  • Mode: the most common value.

An outlier (a value far from the rest) pulls the mean toward it, but hardly changes the median. That is why house prices are often reported as a median.

"What do I need on the last test?" is a missing-value-from-the-mean question.

See three worked examples

Five numbers have a mean of 12. Four are 10, 14, 9, 15. Find the fifth.
  1. Total: 5 × 12 = 60.
  2. 10 + 14 + 9 + 15 = 48.
  3. 60 − 48.

Answer: 12

Your marks are 78, 85, 72. What do you need on the 4th test for a mean of 80?
  1. Total needed: 320.
  2. 78 + 85 + 72 = 235.
  3. 320 − 235.

Answer: 85

Find the median of 4, 9, 2, 7, 11, 3.
  1. Sort: 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 11.
  2. Middle two: 4 and 7. Their mean is 5.5.

Answer: 5.5
