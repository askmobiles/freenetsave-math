Solving proportions

A proportion says two ratios are equal. Solve it by scaling or by cross-multiplying.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A proportion is an equation like x/12 = 5/6. It means the two ratios are equivalent.

Method 1: scale

6 × 2 = 12, so multiply the top by 2 as well: x = 10.

Method 2: cross-multiply

If a/b = c/d, then a × d = b × c. For x/12 = 5/6: 6x = 60, so x = 10.

Setting one up

Keep the same units in the same places. "3 cups of flour make 24 cookies. How many cups for 40 cookies?" → 3/24 = x/40, so 24x = 120 and x = 5.

Check that it makes sense: more cookies should need more flour.

See three worked examples

x/15 = 4/5
  1. 5 × 3 = 15, so x = 4 × 3.

Answer: 12

7/x = 21/30
  1. Cross-multiply: 21x = 210.
  2. x = 10.

Answer: 10

A car uses 6 L of gas per 80 km. How much for 200 km?
  1. 6/80 = x/200.
  2. 80x = 1200, x = 15.

Answer: 15 L
