Similar triangles

Similar triangles have the same shape: equal angles and sides in the same ratio.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two triangles are similar if their matching angles are equal. Then all matching sides grow by the same scale factor.

Here each side of the big triangle is 2 times the matching small side: 6 → 12 and 4 → 8.

Finding a missing side

  1. Match the sides carefully (shortest with shortest, and so on).
  2. Find the scale factor from one known pair: big ÷ small.
  3. Multiply (or divide) the other side by it.

Shadows: at the same time of day, a person and a tree make similar triangles with their shadows, so you can measure a tall tree without climbing it.

See three worked examples

Sides 3 and 5 match sides 12 and ?
  1. Scale factor: 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
  2. 5 × 4 = 20.

Answer: 20

Sides 10 and 15 match sides 4 and ?
  1. Scale factor: 4 ÷ 10 = 0.4.
  2. 15 × 0.4 = 6.

Answer: 6

A 1.6 m person casts a 2 m shadow. A tree casts a 15 m shadow. How tall is it?
  1. Scale factor: 15 ÷ 2 = 7.5.
  2. 1.6 × 7.5 = 12.

Answer: 12 m
