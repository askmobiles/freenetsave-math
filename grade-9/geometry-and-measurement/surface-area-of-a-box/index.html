Surface area of a rectangular prism

Surface area is the total area of all the faces. A box has three pairs of matching rectangles.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Unfold a box and you get its net: six rectangles in three matching pairs.

SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)

Box 5 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm: 2(15 + 10 + 6) = 2(31) = 62 cm².

Units

Surface area is an area, so it is in square units (cm², m²). Volume is in cubic units (cm³).

Open box (no lid): leave out one top face: SA = lw + 2lh + 2wh.

See three worked examples

Box 4 by 3 by 2
  1. lw = 12, lh = 8, wh = 6.
  2. 2(12 + 8 + 6) = 2(26).

Answer: 52 square units

Cube with side 5 cm
  1. Six equal squares: 6 × 25.

Answer: 150 cm²

Open-top box 10 by 6 by 4 (cm)
  1. Bottom: 60. Two long sides: 2 × 40 = 80. Two short sides: 2 × 24 = 48.
  2. 60 + 80 + 48.

Answer: 188 cm²
