Angles and parallel lines

When a line crosses two parallel lines, it makes pairs of angles that are equal or add to 180°.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A transversal is a line that crosses two other lines. If those two lines are parallel, there are three key angle pairs:

  • Corresponding angles (same position at each crossing, an "F" shape) are equal.
  • Alternate angles (on opposite sides of the transversal, between the lines, a "Z" shape) are equal.
  • Co-interior angles (same side, between the lines, a "C" or "U" shape) add to 180°.

Also: angles on a straight line add to 180°, and vertically opposite angles are equal.

Tip: mark every angle you know on the diagram. One angle usually gives you all eight.

See three worked examples

One angle is 65°. What is its corresponding angle?
  1. Corresponding angles are equal.

Answer: 65°

One angle is 112°. What is its co-interior angle?
  1. Co-interior angles add to 180°.
  2. 180 − 112.

Answer: 68°

An angle is 47°. Find its alternate angle and the angle next to it on the straight line.
  1. Alternate: 47°.
  2. On the line: 180 − 47 = 133°.

Answer: 47° and 133°
