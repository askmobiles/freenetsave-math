Volume of a triangular prism

Any prism's volume is the area of its end shape times its length.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A prism has the same cross-section all the way along. Its volume is V = (area of the end) × (length).

For a triangular prism, the end is a triangle with area ½ × base × height:

V = ½ × b × h × L

A tent with a triangular end 2 m wide and 1.5 m tall, 3 m long: V = ½ × 2 × 1.5 × 3 = 4.5 m³.

Do not mix up the two "heights": h is the height of the triangle; L is the length of the prism.

See three worked examples

Triangle base 4, height 3, prism length 10
  1. Triangle area: ½ × 4 × 3 = 6.
  2. 6 × 10.

Answer: 60 cubic units

Ramp: triangle base 2 m, height 0.5 m, width 1.2 m
  1. Triangle area: ½ × 2 × 0.5 = 0.5.
  2. 0.5 × 1.2.

Answer: 0.6 m³

A chocolate bar box has volume 360 cm³, end triangle base 6 cm and height 5 cm. How long is it?
  1. Triangle area: 15 cm².
  2. 360 ÷ 15.

Answer: 24 cm
