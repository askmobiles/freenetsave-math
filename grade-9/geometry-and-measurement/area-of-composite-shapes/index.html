Area of composite shapes

Break a complicated shape into simple pieces, find each area, then add (or subtract).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A composite shape is made of simple shapes. Two strategies:

  • Add pieces: a house shape = rectangle + triangle on top.
  • Subtract pieces: a frame = big rectangle − the hole.

Total: 40 + 12 = 52 square units.

Area formulas you need

  • Rectangle: l × w
  • Triangle: ½ × b × h
  • Trapezoid: ½(a + b)h
  • Circle: πr² (half circle: πr² ÷ 2)

Label each piece and its measurements before you calculate. Missing lengths can often be found by subtracting known lengths.

See three worked examples

A 10 by 6 rectangle with a triangle on top that is 10 wide and 4 tall.
  1. Rectangle: 60.
  2. Triangle: ½ × 10 × 4 = 20.

Answer: 80

A 12 by 8 rectangle with a 4 by 3 rectangle cut out.
  1. 12 × 8 = 96.
  2. 96 − 12.

Answer: 84

An L-shape: a 10 by 4 rectangle joined to a 4 by 6 rectangle.
  1. 40 + 24.

Answer: 64
