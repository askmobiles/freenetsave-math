The Pythagorean theorem

In a right triangle, the square of the longest side equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

In a right triangle the side across from the right angle is the hypotenuse (c), the longest side. The other two are the legs (a and b).

a² + b² = c²

Check: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5². ✓

Finding the hypotenuse

c = √(a² + b²).

Finding a leg

a = √(c² − b²). Subtract, because c is the biggest.

Useful triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25 and their multiples, like 6-8-10.

See three worked examples

Legs 6 and 8. Find the hypotenuse.
  1. c² = 36 + 64 = 100.
  2. c = √100.

Answer: 10

Hypotenuse 13, one leg 5. Find the other leg.
  1. a² = 169 − 25 = 144.
  2. a = √144.

Answer: 12

A 5 m ladder leans on a wall with its foot 1.4 m out. How high does it reach?
  1. h² = 5² − 1.4² = 25 − 1.96 = 23.04.
  2. h = √23.04 = 4.8.

Answer: 4.8 m
