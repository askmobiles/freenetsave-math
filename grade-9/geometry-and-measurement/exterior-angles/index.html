Exterior angles of polygons

Walk around any polygon and you turn a full circle: the exterior angles always add to 360°.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

An exterior angle is formed by one side and the next side extended. At each corner, interior + exterior = 180° (they form a straight line).

If you walk around the outside of any convex polygon, you turn once completely: the exterior angles add to 360°, no matter how many sides.

Regular polygons

  • Each exterior angle = 360° ÷ n.
  • Each interior angle = 180° − exterior angle.

Regular pentagon: exterior 360 ÷ 5 = 72°, interior 180 − 72 = 108°.

Fast way to count sides: n = 360 ÷ (one exterior angle).

See three worked examples

Each exterior angle of a regular hexagon?
  1. 360 ÷ 6.

Answer: 60°

Each interior angle of a regular 9-sided polygon?
  1. Exterior: 360 ÷ 9 = 40°.
  2. Interior: 180 − 40.

Answer: 140°

A regular polygon has exterior angles of 24°. How many sides?
  1. n = 360 ÷ 24.

Answer: 15
