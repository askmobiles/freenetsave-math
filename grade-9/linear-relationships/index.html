Grade 9 Math: Linear relationships

Tables, graphs and equations of straight lines: points on a line, slope, intercepts, first differences and perpendicular lines. Six lessons with examples, practice, a game, a test and challenges.

  1. 1Points on a line: y = mx + bEvery point on a line makes its equation true. Put in x, and the equation tells you y.
  2. 2Slope of a lineSlope measures how steep a line is: how much it rises for each step to the right.
  3. 3The y-interceptThe y-intercept is where a line crosses the y-axis. In y = mx + b it is the number b.
  4. 4The x-interceptThe x-intercept is where a line crosses the x-axis, where y = 0.
  5. 5First differences and tablesIf the y-values in a table go up or down by the same amount each step, the relation is linear.
  6. 6Parallel and perpendicular linesParallel lines have equal slopes. Perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
