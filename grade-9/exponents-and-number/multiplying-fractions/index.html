Multiplying fractions

To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms. "Of" means multiply.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

a/b × c/d = (a × c)/(b × d). For example, 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2.

Why it works

"Half of a quarter" is 1/2 × 1/4. Cut a quarter of a pizza in half and you get one eighth.

Cancel first

Before multiplying, divide any top and any bottom by a common factor. 4/9 × 3/8: 4 and 8 share 4, 3 and 9 share 3, giving 1/3 × 1/2 = 1/6.

Mixed numbers must be changed to improper fractions first: 1½ × 2/3 = 3/2 × 2/3 = 1.

Dividing: multiply by the reciprocal. 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 3/2.

See three worked examples

3/5 × 2/7
  1. Tops: 3 × 2 = 6. Bottoms: 5 × 7 = 35.

Answer: 6/35

5/6 × 3/10
  1. Cancel 5 with 10 (÷5) and 3 with 6 (÷3): 1/2 × 1/2.

Answer: 1/4

2/3 of 3/8 of a $48 gift card
  1. 2/3 × 3/8 = 6/24 = 1/4.
  2. 1/4 × 48 = 12.

Answer: $12
