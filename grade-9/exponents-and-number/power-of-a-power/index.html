Power of a power

When a power is raised to another power, multiply the exponents.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(x³)² means x³ × x³ = x⁶. In general, (xa)b = xa×b.

Power of a product

Everything inside the brackets gets the outside exponent: (2x)³ = 2³x³ = 8x³, and (3a²b)² = 9a⁴b².

Power of a quotient

(x/y)³ = x³/y³, and (2/3)² = 4/9.

Three laws side by side: multiplying powers → add exponents; dividing powers → subtract; power of a power → multiply.

See three worked examples

(a⁴)³
  1. Multiply exponents: 4 × 3 = 12.

Answer: a¹²

(−2x³)²
  1. (−2)² = 4.
  2. (x³)² = x⁶.

Answer: 4x⁶

(x²)⁵ ÷ x⁴
  1. (x²)⁵ = x¹⁰.
  2. x¹⁰ ÷ x⁴ = x⁶.

Answer: x⁶
