Expanding (ax + b)(cx + d)

Every term times every term, then collect the x terms.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(2x + 3)(3x − 1):

x² term: a × c = 6. x term: ad + bc = 2(−1) + 3(3) = 7. Constant: b × d = −3.

(ax + b)(cx + d) = acx² + (ad + bc)x + bd

Watch the middle: it is the sum of the outside and inside products.

See three worked examples

(3x + 2)(x + 4)
  1. 3x², 12x + 2x = 14x, 8.

Answer: 3x² + 14x + 8

(2x − 5)(2x + 5)
  1. 4x², −10x + 10x = 0, −25.

Answer: 4x² − 25

(4x − 1)(x − 3)
  1. 4x², −12x − x = −13x, 3.

Answer: 4x² − 13x + 3
