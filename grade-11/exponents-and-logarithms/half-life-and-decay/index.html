Exponential decay and half-life

Half-life is the time it takes for half of something to be left.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A 64 g sample with a half-life of 5 years: 64 → 32 → 16 → 8 g after 15 years (three half-lives).

A = A₀ × (1/2)^(t ÷ h)

Decay by a percent

A car worth $30 000 loses 15% a year: V = 30 000 × 0.85ᵗ.

Count half-lives: t ÷ h tells how many times to halve.

See three worked examples

200 mg, half-life 6 h. After 18 h?
  1. 3 half-lives: 200 → 100 → 50 → 25.

Answer: 25 mg

80 g, half-life 10 days. After 40 days?
  1. 80 ÷ 2⁴.

Answer: 5 g

A medicine leaves 20% of itself each hour. Factor?
  1. 1 − 0.2.

Answer: × 0.8 per hour
