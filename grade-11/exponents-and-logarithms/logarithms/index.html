Introduction to logarithms

A logarithm answers: "What power do I need?"

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

log₂ 8 asks: 2 to what power gives 8? Since 2³ = 8, log₂ 8 = 3.

log_b(x) = y means bʸ = x

The log graph is the exponential graph reflected in the line y = x: they are inverses.

Facts

  • log_b 1 = 0 (because b⁰ = 1)
  • log_b b = 1
  • log₁₀ is often written just "log". log 1000 = 3.

Real life: the Richter scale for earthquakes and decibels for sound are logarithmic. Each step of 1 on the Richter scale is 10 times stronger.
