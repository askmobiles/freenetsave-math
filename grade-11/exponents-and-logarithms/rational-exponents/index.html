Fraction (rational) exponents

The bottom of the fraction is the root. The top is the power.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Why is 9^(1/2) = 3? Because (9^(1/2))² = 9^1 = 9, so 9^(1/2) is the square root of 9.

x^(1/n) = ⁿ√x    x^(m/n) = (ⁿ√x)ᵐ

Root first, then power

27^(2/3): cube root of 27 is 3, then 3² = 9. Taking the root first keeps the numbers small.

Negative fraction powers

16^(−3/4) = 1 ÷ 16^(3/4) = 1 ÷ 2³ = 1/8.

Remember: denominator = root ("roots are underground").

See three worked examples

  1. √25.

Answer: 5

8^(2/3)
  1. ∛8 = 2.
  2. 2² = 4.

Answer: 4

32^(3/5)
  1. Fifth root of 32 is 2.
  2. 2³ = 8.

Answer: 8
