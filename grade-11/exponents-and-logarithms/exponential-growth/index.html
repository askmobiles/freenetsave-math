Exponential growth

Exponential growth multiplies by the same number in each time period.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A colony of 100 bacteria doubles every hour: 100, 200, 400, 800… After t hours: N = 100 × 2ᵗ.

Compare with linear growth (the grey line), which adds the same amount each step. Exponential growth starts slowly, then shoots up.

Growth by a percent

A town of 20 000 growing 3% a year: P = 20 000 × 1.03ᵗ.

A = A₀ × (growth factor)ᵗ

Doubling every 3 hours? Use t ÷ 3 as the exponent: N = N₀ × 2^(t/3).

See three worked examples

50 cells triple each day. After 4 days?
  1. 50 × 3⁴ = 50 × 81.

Answer: 4050

$1000 grows 5% a year for 2 years
  1. 1000 × 1.05².

Answer: $1102.50

20 bacteria double every 30 min. After 2 hours?
  1. 4 doublings: 20 × 2⁴.

Answer: 320
