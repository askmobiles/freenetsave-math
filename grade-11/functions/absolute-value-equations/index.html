Absolute value equations

|stuff| = b means stuff = b or stuff = −b. Usually two answers.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

|x − 3| = 5 means x − 3 is 5 away from 0. So x − 3 = 5 or x − 3 = −5. x = 8 or x = −2.

Think of it as distance: the numbers 5 away from 3 are 8 and −2.

Special cases

  • |x| = 0 has one answer: 0.
  • |x| = −4 has no answer: distance cannot be negative.

Get the bars alone first: 2|x + 1| = 10 → |x + 1| = 5.

See three worked examples

|x| = 7
  1. x = 7 or −7.

Answer: ±7

|x + 4| = 2
  1. x + 4 = 2 or −2.

Answer: x = −2 or −6

|2x − 1| = 9
  1. 2x − 1 = 9 or −9.

Answer: x = 5 or −4
