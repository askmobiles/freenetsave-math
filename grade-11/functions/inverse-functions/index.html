Inverse functions

The inverse undoes the function. Swap x and y, then solve for y.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

f(x) = 2x + 3 takes 4 to 11. The inverse f⁻¹ takes 11 back to 4.

Finding it

  1. Write y = 2x + 3.
  2. Swap: x = 2y + 3.
  3. Solve for y: y = (x − 3)/2.

So f⁻¹(x) = (x − 3)/2.

The two graphs are mirror images in the line y = x (grey). Every point (a, b) on f becomes (b, a) on f⁻¹.

f⁻¹ is not 1/f. The −1 means "inverse", not a power.

See three worked examples

f(x) = x − 7
  1. Swap: x = y − 7.

Answer: f⁻¹(x) = x + 7

f(x) = 5x
  1. x = 5y.

Answer: f⁻¹(x) = x/5

f(x) = 4x − 1. f⁻¹(11)?
  1. Solve 4x − 1 = 11.

Answer: 3
