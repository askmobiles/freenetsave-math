Grade 11 Math: Trigonometry

Angles beyond 90°: the unit circle, exact values, reference and coterminal angles, radians, sine graphs and the sine area formula. Seven lessons with diagrams.

  1. 1Exact values and the unit circleOn the unit circle, the point at angle θ is (cos θ, sin θ).
  2. 2Reference anglesThe reference angle is the acute angle between the terminal arm and the x-axis.
  3. 3Coterminal anglesAdd or subtract full turns (360°) to get an angle that ends in the same place.
  4. 4Radian measureA full turn is 2π radians, so π radians = 180°.
  5. 5Converting degrees to radiansMultiply degrees by π/180 and simplify the fraction.
  6. 6Sine graphs: amplitude and periodIn y = a sin(kx), the amplitude is |a| and the period is 360° ÷ k.
  7. 7Area of a triangle using sineArea = ½ × a × b × sin C, where C is the angle between sides a and b.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
