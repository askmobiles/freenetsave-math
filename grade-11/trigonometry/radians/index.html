Radian measure

A full turn is 2π radians, so π radians = 180°.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A radian is the angle that makes an arc as long as the radius. A full circle has circumference 2πr, so a full turn is 2π radians = 360°.

π radians = 180°

Radians to degrees

Replace π with 180°: π/3 = 180°/3 = 60°. 3π/4 = 3 × 45° = 135°.

Without π: 1 radian = 180/π ≈ 57.3°.

Why radians? Arc length is just s = rθ, which makes many formulas in calculus and physics simpler.
