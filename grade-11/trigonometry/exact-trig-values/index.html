Exact values and the unit circle

On the unit circle, the point at angle θ is (cos θ, sin θ).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The unit circle has radius 1. Turn θ counter-clockwise from the positive x-axis. The point you land on is (cos θ, sin θ).

At 150°: x is negative, y is positive. sin 150° = 0.5, cos 150° ≈ −0.866.

Signs by quadrant (CAST)

Quadrant 1: All positive. 2: Sin. 3: Tan. 4: Cos.

tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ. tan 90° is undefined (dividing by 0).
