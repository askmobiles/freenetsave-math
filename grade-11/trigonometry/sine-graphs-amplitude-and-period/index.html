Sine graphs: amplitude and period

In y = a sin(kx), the amplitude is |a| and the period is 360° ÷ k.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

y = sin x is a wave that repeats every 360°. It goes up to 1 and down to −1.

  • Amplitude |a|: the height of the wave above its middle line. 3 sin 2x has amplitude 3.
  • Period 360° ÷ k: the length of one full wave. 3 sin 2x repeats every 180°.

Real life: tides, sound waves, Ferris wheels and daylight hours over a year all follow sine waves.

See three worked examples

y = 5 sin x
  1. a = 5, k = 1.

Answer: amplitude 5, period 360°

y = 2 sin 3x
  1. 360 ÷ 3.

Answer: amplitude 2, period 120°

y = −4 sin (x/2)
  1. |−4| = 4; 360 ÷ ½ = 720.

Answer: amplitude 4, period 720°
