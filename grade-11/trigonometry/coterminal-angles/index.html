Coterminal angles

Add or subtract full turns (360°) to get an angle that ends in the same place.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Turning 30° and turning 390° (a full turn plus 30°) end at the same spot. They are coterminal.

  • Positive angles turn counter-clockwise.
  • Negative angles turn clockwise: −90° ends where 270° ends.

θ ± 360°k

To find the coterminal angle between 0° and 360°: subtract 360 until it is small enough, or add 360 to a negative angle.

Coterminal angles have the same sin, cos and tan. sin 390° = sin 30° = ½.

See three worked examples

Coterminal with 400° (0°–360°)
  1. 400 − 360.

Answer: 40°

Coterminal with −45°
  1. −45 + 360.

Answer: 315°

Coterminal with 800°
  1. 800 − 720.

Answer: 80°
