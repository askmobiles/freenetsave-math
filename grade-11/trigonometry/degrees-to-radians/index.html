Converting degrees to radians

Multiply degrees by π/180 and simplify the fraction.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

120° × π/180 = 120π/180 = 2π/3.

Fast way

Know that 30° = π/6, 45° = π/4, 60° = π/3, 90° = π/2. Build others from these: 150° = 5 × 30° = 5π/6.
