Compounding periods

Monthly compounding: rate ÷ 12, periods = years × 12.

6% a year compounded monthly for 3 years: i = 0.06 ÷ 12 = 0.005, n = 3 × 12 = 36. A = P(1.005)³⁶.

$1000 at 12% a year: yearly → $1120.00; monthly → 1000 × 1.01¹² ≈ $1126.83. More often = a little more money.

Mortgages in Canada are compounded semi-annually by law, even though you pay monthly.

See three worked examples

8% compounded quarterly: i?
  1. 0.08 ÷ 4.

Answer: 0.02 (2% per quarter)

Monthly for 5 years: n?
  1. 5 × 12.

Answer: 60

$2000 at 6% semi-annually for 1 year
  1. i = 0.03, n = 2.
  2. 2000 × 1.0609.

Answer: $2121.80
