Compound interest

A = P(1 + i)ⁿ. Interest earns interest.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

$1000 at 10% a year, compounded yearly: 1000 → 1100 → 1210 → 1331. Each year adds 10% of the new balance.

A = P(1 + i)ⁿ

  • P = principal, i = interest rate per period (decimal), n = number of periods.
  • Interest earned = A − P.

$5000 at 4% for 10 years: A = 5000 × 1.04¹⁰ ≈ $7401.22.

Start early: after 20 years at 10%, compound interest gives over twice as much as simple interest.

See three worked examples

$2000 at 5% for 3 years
  1. 2000 × 1.05³ = 2000 × 1.157625.

Answer: $2315.25

Interest earned on $800 at 10% for 2 years
  1. 800 × 1.21 = 968.

Answer: $168

$10 000 at 3% for 5 years
  1. 10 000 × 1.159274.

Answer: $11 592.74
