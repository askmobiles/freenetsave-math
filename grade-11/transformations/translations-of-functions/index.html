Translations: shifting a graph

y = f(x − h) + k moves every point h to the right and k up.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Start with y = √x (blue). y = √(x − 3) + 2 (orange) is the same shape moved 3 right and 2 up.

(x, y) → (x + h, y + k)

  • f(x − 3): 3 right (the sign looks backwards).
  • f(x + 3): 3 left.
  • f(x) + 2: 2 up. f(x) − 2: 2 down.

Inside the brackets changes x (opposite sign). Outside changes y (same sign).

See three worked examples

(2, 5) on f. Where on f(x − 4)?
  1. 4 right.

Answer: (6, 5)

(2, 5) on f. Where on f(x + 1) − 3?
  1. 1 left, 3 down.

Answer: (1, 2)

y = x² moved 5 left, 1 up
  1. f(x + 5) + 1.

Answer: y = (x + 5)² + 1
