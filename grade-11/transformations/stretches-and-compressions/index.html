Vertical stretches and compressions

y = a f(x) multiplies every y-value by a.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

y = 2√x (orange) is twice as tall as y = √x (blue). y = ½√x (green) is half as tall.

(x, y) → (x, a·y)

  • a > 1: vertical stretch (taller).
  • 0 < a < 1: vertical compression (flatter).
  • Points on the x-axis (y = 0) do not move.

Horizontal stretches

y = f(kx) changes x instead: (x, y) → (x/k, y). y = f(2x) squeezes the graph toward the y-axis by half.

Order: stretch first, then shift.

See three worked examples

(3, 4) on f. On 3f(x)?
  1. y × 3.

Answer: (3, 12)

(6, −8) on f. On ½f(x)?
  1. y × ½.

Answer: (6, −4)

(6, −8) on f. On f(2x)?
  1. x ÷ 2.

Answer: (3, −8)
