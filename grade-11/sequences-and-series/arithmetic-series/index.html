Arithmetic series

Sum = number of terms × (first + last) ÷ 2.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A series is the sum of a sequence. Add 1 + 2 + 3 + … + 100.

Pair them up: 1 + 100 = 101, 2 + 99 = 101, … There are 50 pairs, so the sum is 50 × 101 = 5050.

Sₙ = n(t₁ + tₙ) ÷ 2   or   Sₙ = n[2a + (n − 1)d] ÷ 2

3 + 7 + 11 + … (20 terms): last term = 3 + 19 × 4 = 79. S = 20 × 82 ÷ 2 = 820.

Average × count: (first + last) ÷ 2 is the average term.

See three worked examples

2 + 4 + 6 + … + 40
  1. 20 terms.
  2. 20 × 42 ÷ 2.

Answer: 420

5 + 10 + 15 + … (12 terms)
  1. Last = 60.
  2. 12 × 65 ÷ 2.

Answer: 390

Sum of the first 50 odd numbers
  1. Last = 99.
  2. 50 × 100 ÷ 2.

Answer: 2500
