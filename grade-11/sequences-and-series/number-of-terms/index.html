How many terms?

Number of terms = (last − first) ÷ d + 1.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

How many terms in 7, 11, 15, …, 95?

Set tₙ = 95: 7 + (n − 1)4 = 95 → (n − 1)4 = 88 → n − 1 = 22 → n = 23.

n = (last − first) ÷ d + 1

The fence-post problem

A 20 m fence with posts every 2 m needs 20 ÷ 2 + 1 = 11 posts, not 10. That "+1" is the same idea.

Check: if n is not a whole number, the last number is not in the sequence.

See three worked examples

3, 8, 13, …, 98
  1. (98 − 3) ÷ 5 + 1.

Answer: 20 terms

100, 94, 88, …, 4
  1. (4 − 100) ÷ (−6) + 1.

Answer: 17 terms

Even numbers from 10 to 200
  1. (200 − 10) ÷ 2 + 1.

Answer: 96
