Arc length and radians

Arc length s = r × θ, with θ in radians.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

One radian is the angle whose arc equals the radius. So an angle of θ radians has an arc of θ radii.

s = rθ   θ = s ÷ r

Radius 8 cm, angle 2.5 rad: s = 20 cm.

Degrees first?

Change to radians: 60° = π/3. Radius 9: s = 9 × π/3 = 3π ≈ 9.42.

Area of a sector: A = ½r²θ.
