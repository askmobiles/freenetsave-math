The number e and natural logarithms

ln is the log with base e ≈ 2.718. ln and eˣ undo each other.

e ≈ 2.71828 appears whenever something grows continuously. $1 at 100% interest compounded more and more often approaches $e.

Continuous growth: A = Pe^(rt). $1000 at 5% for 10 years: 1000e^0.5 ≈ $1648.72.

ln 1 = 0 and ln e = 1, just like any log.
