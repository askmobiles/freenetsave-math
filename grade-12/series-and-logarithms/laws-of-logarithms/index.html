Laws of logarithms

Logs turn multiplying into adding and powers into multiplying.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

LawRuleExample
Productlog a + log b = log(ab)log₂ 4 + log₂ 8 = log₂ 32 = 5
Quotientlog a − log b = log(a/b)log₃ 54 − log₃ 2 = log₃ 27 = 3
Powerlog aⁿ = n log alog₅ 25³ = 3 log₅ 25 = 6

They come from the exponent laws: multiplying powers adds exponents, and a log is an exponent.

Change of base

log_b x = log x ÷ log b. Use it to work out log₇ 50 on a calculator.

Not a law: log(a + b) is NOT log a + log b.
