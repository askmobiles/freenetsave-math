Infinite geometric series

If −1 < r < 1, the infinite sum is a ÷ (1 − r).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

½ + ¼ + ⅛ + … never stops, but the total gets closer and closer to 1. Picture cutting a square in half, then half the rest, and so on.

S∞ = a ÷ (1 − r), only when −1 < r < 1

12 + 4 + 4/3 + …: a = 12, r = 1/3. S = 12 ÷ (2/3) = 18.

Repeating decimals

0.333… = 3/10 + 3/100 + … = (3/10) ÷ (9/10) = 1/3.

If |r| ≥ 1, the terms do not shrink and the sum has no limit.
