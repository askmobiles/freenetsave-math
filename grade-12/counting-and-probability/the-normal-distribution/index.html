The normal distribution

About 68% of data is within 1 standard deviation of the mean, 95% within 2, and 99.7% within 3.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Heights, test scores and measuring errors often form a bell-shaped (normal) curve: most values near the mean, fewer far away.

WithinPercent of data
1 standard deviation68%
2 standard deviations95%
3 standard deviations99.7%

Test scores: mean 70, standard deviation 8. About 68% scored between 62 and 78; 95% between 54 and 86.

Symmetric: half the data is above the mean, half below.

See three worked examples

Mean 100, σ = 15. Middle 95%?
  1. 100 ± 30.

Answer: 70 to 130

Same. Percent above 115?
  1. (100 − 68) ÷ 2.

Answer: 16%

Same. Percent between 100 and 130?
  1. 95 ÷ 2.

Answer: 47.5%
