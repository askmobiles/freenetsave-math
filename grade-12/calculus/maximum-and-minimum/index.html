Maximum and minimum points

At a highest or lowest point the tangent is flat: f′(x) = 0.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

f(x) = x² − 8x + 3: f′(x) = 2x − 8 = 0 at x = 4. Since the parabola opens up, it is a minimum: f(4) = −13.

Cubic example

y = x³ − 3x: y′ = 3x² − 3 = 0 at x = ±1. y″ = 6x: at x = −1, y″ < 0 (max); at x = 1, y″ > 0 (min).

Optimization

A farmer has 40 m of fence for a rectangle. Area A = x(20 − x) = 20x − x². A′ = 20 − 2x = 0 at x = 10. Best: a 10 m by 10 m square, 100 m².

Also check the ends of the allowed interval: the biggest value might be there.

f(x) = x³ − 12x. Critical points?
  1. 3x² − 12 = 0.

Answer: x = ±2

Two numbers add to 30. Largest product?
  1. x(30 − x), derivative 30 − 2x.

Answer: 15 × 15 = 225
