Velocity and acceleration

Position s(t). Velocity v = s′(t). Acceleration a = v′(t) = s″(t).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A car's position is s(t) = 2t² + 3t metres.

  • Velocity: v(t) = 4t + 3. At t = 5 s, v = 23 m/s.
  • Acceleration: a(t) = 4 m/s², the same at every moment.

Signs

  • v > 0: moving forward. v < 0: moving backward. v = 0: stopped (for an instant).
  • Speeding up when v and a have the same sign.

Dropped object: s = −4.9t² gives v = −9.8t and a = −9.8 m/s² (gravity).

See three worked examples

s = t³ − 6t. v(2)?
  1. 3t² − 6 = 6.

Answer: 6 m/s

Same. a(2)?
  1. 6t = 12.

Answer: 12 m/s²

s = 20t − 5t². When does it stop?
  1. 20 − 10t = 0.

Answer: t = 2 s
