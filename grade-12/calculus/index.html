Grade 12 Math: Calculus

Limits, derivatives and their rules, tangent lines, maximum and minimum, motion, and areas under curves with definite integrals. Thirteen lessons with graphs.

  1. 1LimitsA limit asks: what value does f(x) get close to as x gets close to a number?
  2. 2The derivative: slope of a curveThe derivative f′(x) gives the slope of the curve at each point.
  3. 3The power ruleBring the power down in front, then lower the power by 1.
  4. 4Equation of a tangent lineSlope = f′(a). Point = (a, f(a)). Then y = mx + b.
  5. 5The product rule(first × second)′ = first′ × second + first × second′.
  6. 6The chain ruleDerivative of the outside (leave the inside alone) × derivative of the inside.
  7. 7The second derivative and concavityf″(x) is the derivative of the derivative. It tells you how the slope is changing.
  8. 8Derivatives of exponential functionseˣ is its own derivative. For eᵏˣ, multiply by k.
  9. 9Derivatives of sin x and cos xsin x → cos x → −sin x → −cos x → back to sin x.
  10. 10Maximum and minimum pointsAt a highest or lowest point the tangent is flat: f′(x) = 0.
  11. 11Velocity and accelerationPosition s(t). Velocity v = s′(t). Acceleration a = v′(t) = s″(t).
  12. 12Area under a lineThe area under y = mx from 0 to b is a triangle: ½ × b × mb.
  13. 13Definite integralsFind an antiderivative F, then work out F(b) − F(a).
