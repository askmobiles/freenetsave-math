The product rule

(first × second)′ = first′ × second + first × second′.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(fg)′ = f′g + fg′

y = (2x + 1)(x² − 3):

  • f = 2x + 1, f′ = 2
  • g = x² − 3, g′ = 2x

y′ = 2(x² − 3) + (2x + 1)(2x) = 2x² − 6 + 4x² + 2x = 6x² + 2x − 6.

Check

Expand first: 2x³ + x² − 6x − 3, derivative 6x² + 2x − 6 ✓.

Not f′ × g′. That is the most common mistake.

See three worked examples

y = x(x + 5)
  1. 1(x + 5) + x(1).

Answer: 2x + 5

y = x²(3x − 1)
  1. 2x(3x − 1) + x²(3).

Answer: 9x² − 2x

y = (x + 1)(x − 2) at x = 3
  1. (x − 2) + (x + 1) = 2x − 1.

Answer: 5
