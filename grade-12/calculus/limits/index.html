Limits

A limit asks: what value does f(x) get close to as x gets close to a number?

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

f(x) = (x² − 9)/(x − 3) cannot be worked out at x = 3 (0/0). But near 3 it is very close to 6:

x2.92.993.013.1
f(x)5.95.996.016.1

So lim (x→3) of (x² − 9)/(x − 3) = 6. The graph is the line y = x + 3 with a hole at (3, 6).

Method

  1. Try plugging in. If you get a number, that is the limit.
  2. If you get 0/0, factor and cancel: (x − 3)(x + 3)/(x − 3) = x + 3.
  3. Plug in again: 3 + 3 = 6.

Why limits matter: the derivative is defined as a limit.

See three worked examples

lim x→2 of (x² − 4)/(x − 2)
  1. (x + 2) after cancelling.

Answer: 4

lim x→5 of (3x + 1)
  1. Plug in.

Answer: 16

lim x→1 of (x² + 2x − 3)/(x − 1)
  1. (x + 3)(x − 1)/(x − 1).

Answer: 4
