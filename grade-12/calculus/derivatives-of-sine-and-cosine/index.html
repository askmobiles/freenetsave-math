Derivatives of sin x and cos x

sin x → cos x → −sin x → −cos x → back to sin x.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Look at the slope of y = sin x (blue): steepest at x = 0 (slope 1), flat at π/2 (slope 0). That pattern is exactly cos x (orange).

(sin x)′ = cos x   (cos x)′ = −sin x

With the chain rule: (sin 3x)′ = 3 cos 3x. (5 cos x)′ = −5 sin x.

Radians only. These rules are only true when x is in radians.

See three worked examples

(4 sin x)′ at x = 0
  1. 4 cos 0 = 4.

Answer: 4

(cos x)′ at x = 0
  1. −sin 0.

Answer: 0

(sin 2x)′
  1. Chain rule.

Answer: 2 cos 2x
