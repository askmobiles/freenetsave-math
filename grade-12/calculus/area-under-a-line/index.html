Area under a line

The area under y = mx from 0 to b is a triangle: ½ × b × mb.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Area under y = 2x from x = 0 to x = 3: a triangle with base 3 and height 6. Area = ½ × 3 × 6 = 9.

The integral way

∫₀³ 2x dx: the antiderivative of 2x is x². Evaluate: 3² − 0² = 9. Same answer!

∫₀ᵇ mx dx = mb²/2

Meaning: area under a velocity–time graph is the distance travelled.

See three worked examples

∫₀⁴ 2x dx
  1. x² from 0 to 4.

Answer: 16

∫₀⁵ 6x dx
  1. 3x² → 75.

Answer: 75

Velocity v = 10t m/s for 3 s. Distance?
  1. ∫₀³ 10t dt = 5t² = 45.

Answer: 45 m
