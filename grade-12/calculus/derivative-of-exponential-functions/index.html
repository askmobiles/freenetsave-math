Derivatives of exponential functions

eˣ is its own derivative. For eᵏˣ, multiply by k.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The function eˣ is special: at every point, its slope equals its height.

d/dx eˣ = eˣ   d/dx eᵏˣ = k·eᵏˣ

5e^(3x) → 15e^(3x). e^(−2x) → −2e^(−2x).

Other bases

d/dx bˣ = bˣ ln b. So 2ˣ → 2ˣ ln 2.

Growth models: if P = P₀e^(kt), then P′ = kP — the growth rate is proportional to the size.
