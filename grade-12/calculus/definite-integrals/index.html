Definite integrals

Find an antiderivative F, then work out F(b) − F(a).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

An antiderivative undoes a derivative. The antiderivative of xⁿ is xⁿ⁺¹ ÷ (n + 1).

∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a)

∫₀² 3x² dx: antiderivative x³. 2³ − 0³ = 8. That is the area under the curve from 0 to 2.

Term by term

∫₁³ (2x + 1) dx = [x² + x] from 1 to 3 = (9 + 3) − (1 + 1) = 10.

Area below the x-axis counts as negative in an integral.

See three worked examples

∫₀³ x² dx
  1. x³/3 → 9.

Answer: 9

∫₁² 4x³ dx
  1. x⁴: 16 − 1.

Answer: 15

∫₀¹ (x + 1) dx
  1. x²/2 + x → ½ + 1.

Answer: 3/2
