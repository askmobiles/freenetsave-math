Equation of a tangent line

Slope = f′(a). Point = (a, f(a)). Then y = mx + b.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Steps for y = x² at x = 3

  1. Point: f(3) = 9 → (3, 9).
  2. Slope: f′(x) = 2x → f′(3) = 6.
  3. Equation: 9 = 6(3) + b → b = −9. Tangent: y = 6x − 9.

Normal line: perpendicular to the tangent, slope −1/m.

See three worked examples

y = x² at x = 1

  2. b = −1.

Answer: y = 2x − 1

y = x³ at x = 2
  1. (2, 8), slope 12.
  2. b = −16.

Answer: y = 12x − 16

y = 3x² at x = −1
  1. (−1, 3), slope −6.
  2. b = −3.

Answer: y = −6x − 3
