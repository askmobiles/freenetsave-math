The chain rule

Derivative of the outside (leave the inside alone) × derivative of the inside.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

y = (3x + 1)⁵. The outside is ( )⁵, the inside is 3x + 1.

y′ = 5(3x + 1)⁴ × 3 = 15(3x + 1)⁴

Why

If the inside changes 3 times as fast as x, the whole thing changes 3 times as fast too. Rates multiply, like gears.

More examples

  • √(x² + 1) → ½(x² + 1)^(−1/2) × 2x = x/√(x² + 1)
  • (1 − 2x)³ → 3(1 − 2x)² × (−2) = −6(1 − 2x)²

Do not forget the inside derivative — that is the step people miss.

See three worked examples

(2x + 3)²
  1. 2(2x + 3) × 2.

Answer: 4(2x + 3) = 8x + 12

(x² − 1)³
  1. 3(x² − 1)² × 2x.

Answer: 6x(x² − 1)²

(5x − 2)⁴ at x = 0
  1. 4(5x − 2)³ × 5 = 20(−8).

Answer: −160
