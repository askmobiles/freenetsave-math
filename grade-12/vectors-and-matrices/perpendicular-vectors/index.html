Perpendicular vectors

u ⊥ v exactly when u · v = 0.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(2, 1) · (−1, 2) = −2 + 2 = 0, so these vectors meet at a right angle.

Quick trick

To get a vector perpendicular to (a, b), swap the parts and change one sign: (−b, a). For (5, 3): (−3, 5).

Finding a missing part

(3, k) ⊥ (4, −6): 12 − 6k = 0 → k = 2.

Use: checking for right angles in triangles drawn on a grid.

See three worked examples

Is (3, 6) ⊥ (4, −2)?
  1. 12 − 12 = 0.

Answer: yes

Is (1, 5) ⊥ (5, 1)?
  1. 5 + 5 = 10.

Answer: no

(k, 2) ⊥ (3, 6). k?
  1. 3k + 12 = 0.

Answer: k = −4
