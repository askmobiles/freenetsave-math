Vectors and adding vectors

A vector has size and direction. Add vectors by adding matching parts.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A vector like (3, 1) means "3 right and 1 up". It has a length and a direction, but no fixed starting point.

blue: u · orange: v · green: u + v = (5, 5)

Tip-to-tail: start v where u ends. The sum goes from the start of u to the end of v.

(a, b) + (c, d) = (a + c, b + d)

(3, 1) + (2, 4) = (5, 5).

Real life: a plane flying north at 300 km/h in a 50 km/h east wind actually moves along (50, 300).

See three worked examples

(4, −2) + (1, 6)
  1. (4 + 1, −2 + 6).

Answer: (5, 4)

(−3, 5) − (2, 1)
  1. Subtract parts.

Answer: (−5, 4)

(1, 2, 3) + (4, 0, −1)
  1. 3D works the same.

Answer: (5, 2, 2)
