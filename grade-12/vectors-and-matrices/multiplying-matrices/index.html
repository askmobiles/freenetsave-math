Multiplying matrices

Each entry of the product is a row of the first matrix dotted with a column of the second.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

[1 2; 3 4] × [5 6; 7 8]:

  • Top-left: row 1 · column 1 = 1 × 5 + 2 × 7 = 19
  • Top-right: row 1 · column 2 = 1 × 6 + 2 × 8 = 22
  • Bottom-left: row 2 · column 1 = 3 × 5 + 4 × 7 = 43
  • Bottom-right: row 2 · column 2 = 3 × 6 + 4 × 8 = 50

Product = [19 22; 43 50]

Order matters

A × B is usually not the same as B × A.

Identity matrix [1 0; 0 1] works like the number 1: A × I = A.

See three worked examples
