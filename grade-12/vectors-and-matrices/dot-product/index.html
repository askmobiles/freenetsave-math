The dot product

u · v = (first × first) + (second × second). The answer is a number, not a vector.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(2, 3) · (4, −1) = 2 × 4 + 3 × (−1) = 8 − 3 = 5.

Angle between vectors

u · v = |u| |v| cos θ

  • Positive dot product: angle less than 90°.
  • Zero: exactly 90° (perpendicular).
  • Negative: more than 90°.

(1, 0) · (1, 1) = 1; |u| = 1, |v| = √2; cos θ = 1/√2, θ = 45°.

Work: in physics, work = force · displacement — a dot product.

See three worked examples

  1. 6 + 5.

Answer: 11

(−2, 4) · (3, 1)
  1. −6 + 4.

Answer: −2

(1, 2, 3) · (4, 5, 6)
  1. 4 + 10 + 18.

Answer: 32
