Surface area of a square pyramid

Surface area = base + four triangles. Each triangle is ½ × base × slant height.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Unfold a square pyramid: one square in the middle, four triangles around it.

SA = s² + 4 × (½ × s × ℓ) = s² + 2sℓ

ℓ is the slant height: the height of each triangle face, not the height of the pyramid.

s = 6, ℓ = 5: SA = 36 + 2(6)(5) = 36 + 60 = 96.

Finding ℓ: if you know the pyramid's height h, then ℓ² = h² + (s/2)².

See three worked examples

s = 10, ℓ = 12
  1. 100 + 2(10)(12) = 100 + 240.

Answer: 340

s = 4, ℓ = 3
  1. 16 + 24.

Answer: 40

s = 8, height 3. Find ℓ, then SA.
  1. ℓ² = 9 + 16 = 25, ℓ = 5.
  2. 64 + 80.

Answer: 144
