Earning commission

Commission is a percent of what you sell. Many jobs pay a base amount plus commission.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Commission = rate × sales. A car salesperson on 3% commission who sells a $30 000 car earns 0.03 × 30 000 = $900.

Base plus commission

Pay = base + rate × sales. $400 a week plus 5% of $8000 sales = 400 + 400 = $800.

Graduated commission

Some jobs pay 4% on the first $10 000 and 6% on anything above. Sales of $15 000: 400 + 0.06 × 5000 = 400 + 300 = $700.

Pros and cons: commission rewards hard work, but pay goes up and down with sales.
