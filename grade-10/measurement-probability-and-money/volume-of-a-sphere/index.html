Volume and surface area of a sphere

A sphere's volume is 4πr³ ÷ 3 and its surface area is 4πr².

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

V = 4πr³ ÷ 3    SA = 4πr²

r = 3: V = 4 × π × 27 ÷ 3 = 36π ≈ 113.1. SA = 4π × 9 = 36π ≈ 113.1. (Same number only when r = 3!)

A neat fact

A sphere fits inside a cylinder of the same width and height and fills exactly ⅔ of it.

Cube the radius for volume (cubic units); square it for surface area (square units).
