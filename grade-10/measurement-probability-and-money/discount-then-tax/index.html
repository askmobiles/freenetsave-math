Sales, discounts and HST

Take off the discount first, then add 13% HST to the sale price.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two steps

  1. Sale price = price × (1 − discount). 25% off → × 0.75.
  2. Total = sale price × 1.13 (Ontario HST is 13%).

$80 shoes, 25% off: sale price 80 × 0.75 = $60. With tax: 60 × 1.13 = $67.80.

One step

80 × 0.75 × 1.13 = $67.80. The order of multiplying does not matter.

Comparing deals

"Buy one, get one 50% off" on two $40 items: 40 + 20 = $60, which is 25% off the pair.

Discounts do not add: 20% off, then another 10% off is 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72, which is 28% off, not 30%.

See three worked examples

$120 coat, 30% off, plus HST
  1. 120 × 0.7 = 84.
  2. 84 × 1.13.

Answer: $94.92

$50 game, 10% off, plus HST
  1. 45 × 1.13.

Answer: $50.85

$200 bike, 20% off then 10% off, before tax
  1. 200 × 0.8 × 0.9.

Answer: $144
