Volume of a cone

A cone holds one third of the cylinder with the same base and height.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Fill a cone with water three times and pour it into a cylinder of the same radius and height: it fills exactly.

V = πr²h ÷ 3

r = 3, h = 10: V = π × 9 × 10 ÷ 3 = 30π ≈ 94.2.

Diameter given? Halve it to get r first.

See three worked examples

r = 6, h = 5
  1. π × 36 × 5 ÷ 3.

Answer: 60π ≈ 188.5

Diameter 8, h = 9
  1. r = 4. π × 16 × 9 ÷ 3.

Answer: 48π ≈ 150.8

A cone has volume 12π and r = 3. h?
  1. 12π = 9πh ÷ 3 = 3πh.

Answer: h = 4
