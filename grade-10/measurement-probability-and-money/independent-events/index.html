Probability: picking with replacement

If the first pick goes back, the second pick is just like the first. Multiply.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A bag has 2 red and 3 blue. Pick one, put it back, pick again. The picks are independent: the first does not change the second.

P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

P(both red) = 2/5 × 2/5 = 4/25.

Check: all the end branches add to 1: 4 + 6 + 6 + 9 = 25 out of 25.

See three worked examples

Bag: 3 red, 1 blue. P(red, then red) with replacement
  1. 3/4 × 3/4.

Answer: 9/16

Flip a coin twice. P(two heads)
  1. 1/2 × 1/2.

Answer: 1/4

Roll a die and flip a coin. P(6 and heads)
  1. 1/6 × 1/2.

Answer: 1/12
