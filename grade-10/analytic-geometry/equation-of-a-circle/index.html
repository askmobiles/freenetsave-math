Equation of a circle

Every point on a circle centred at (0, 0) is the same distance r from the centre, so x² + y² = r².

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Take any point (x, y) on a circle of radius 5 centred at the origin. Its distance from (0, 0) is 5, so by Pythagoras x² + y² = 25.

x² + y² = r²

  • x² + y² = 49 → r = 7.
  • Radius 9 → x² + y² = 81.

Do not forget the square root: x² + y² = 36 has radius 6, not 36.

Equation of the circle with radius 11, centre (0, 0)
  1. 11² = 121.

Answer: x² + y² = 121

Equation of the circle through (6, 8), centre (0, 0)
  1. r² = 36 + 64 = 100.

Answer: x² + y² = 100
