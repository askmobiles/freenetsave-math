Distance between two points

The distance between two points is the hypotenuse of a right triangle drawn on the grid.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

From (1, 1) to (4, 5): go across 3 and up 4. The straight line between them is the hypotenuse: √(3² + 4²) = 5.

d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)

Negative differences are fine: squaring makes them positive.

See three worked examples

(2, 3) to (8, 11)
  1. Across 6, up 8.
  2. √(36 + 64) = √100.

Answer: 10

(−3, 4) to (2, −8)
  1. Across 5, down 12.
  2. √(25 + 144) = √169.

Answer: 13

(0, 0) to (2, 2)
  1. √(4 + 4) = √8.

Answer: √8 ≈ 2.83
