Similar triangles and indirect measurement

Matching sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two triangles are similar if their angles match. Then each side of the big one is the small one's side times the same scale factor k.

Here k = 12 ÷ 6 = 2, so ? = 4 × 2 = 8.

Proportion form

?/4 = 12/6. Cross-multiply: 6 × ? = 48, ? = 8.

Indirect measurement

A 2 m pole casts a 3 m shadow while a tree casts a 15 m shadow. Height of tree = 2 × (15 ÷ 3) = 10 m.

Match the sides carefully: shortest with shortest, longest with longest.

See three worked examples

Sides 5, 8 match 15, ?
  1. k = 3.
  2. 8 × 3.

Answer: 24

Sides 9, 12 match 6, ?
  1. k = 6 ÷ 9 = 2/3.
  2. 12 × 2/3.

Answer: 8

A 1.5 m girl casts a 2 m shadow. A building casts a 30 m shadow. Height?
  1. k = 15.
  2. 1.5 × 15.

Answer: 22.5 m
