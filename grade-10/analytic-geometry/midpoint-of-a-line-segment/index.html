Midpoint of a line segment

The midpoint is the average of the x values and the average of the y values.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

M = ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2)

From (−2, 1) to (6, 5): x = (−2 + 6) ÷ 2 = 2, y = (1 + 5) ÷ 2 = 3. Midpoint (2, 3).

Working backwards

If M is the midpoint of A and B, then B = 2M − A. Midpoint (4, 1), one end (1, −2): other end (7, 4).

Use: midpoints find the centre of a circle from a diameter, or where to put a support halfway along a beam.

See three worked examples

(2, 8) and (6, 4)
  1. (8 ÷ 2, 12 ÷ 2).

Answer: (4, 6)

(−5, 3) and (1, −7)
  1. (−4 ÷ 2, −4 ÷ 2).

Answer: (−2, −2)

M(3, 5), one end (0, 2). Other end?
  1. 2(3) − 0 = 6, 2(5) − 2 = 8.

Answer: (6, 8)
