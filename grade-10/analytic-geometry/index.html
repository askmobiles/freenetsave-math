Grade 10 Math: Analytic geometry

Distance, midpoint, circles centred at the origin and similar triangles. Five lessons with grids, examples, practice, a game, a test and challenges.

  1. 1Distance between two pointsThe distance between two points is the hypotenuse of a right triangle drawn on the grid.
  2. 2Midpoint of a line segmentThe midpoint is the average of the x values and the average of the y values.
  3. 3Equation of a circleEvery point on a circle centred at (0, 0) is the same distance r from the centre, so x² + y² = r².
  4. 4Is a point inside, on or outside a circle?Work out x² + y² and compare it with r².
  5. 5Similar triangles and indirect measurementMatching sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
