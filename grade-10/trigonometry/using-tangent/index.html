Using the tangent ratio

Tangent links the two legs of a right triangle: opposite ÷ adjacent.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

When the problem is about a height and a distance along the ground, those are the two legs, so use tan.

tan A = 3/4 and the adjacent side is 20: opposite = 20 × 3/4 = 15.

Slope and tangent

The slope of a line is rise ÷ run, which is tan of the angle it makes with the x-axis. A slope of 1 is 45°.

Opposite = adjacent × tan A, and adjacent = opposite ÷ tan A.

See three worked examples

tan A = 5/12, adjacent 24. Opposite?
  1. 24 × 5/12.

Answer: 10

tan A = 2, opposite 18. Adjacent?
  1. 18 ÷ 2.

Answer: 9

A road rises 8 m every 100 m along the ground. tan of its angle?
  1. 8 ÷ 100.

Answer: 0.08
