Sine, cosine and tangent

SOH CAH TOA: three ratios of sides in a right triangle.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

In a right triangle, name the sides from the point of view of angle θ:

  • Hypotenuse: the longest side, across from the right angle.
  • Opposite: across from θ.
  • Adjacent: next to θ (not the hypotenuse).
RatioRuleHere
sin θopposite ÷ hypotenuse (SOH)3/5 = 0.6
cos θadjacent ÷ hypotenuse (CAH)4/5 = 0.8
tan θopposite ÷ adjacent (TOA)3/4 = 0.75

Opposite and adjacent swap if you look from the other acute angle.

See three worked examples

Opposite 5, adjacent 12, hypotenuse 13. sin θ?
  1. 5 ÷ 13.

Answer: 5/13

Same triangle. cos θ?
  1. 12 ÷ 13.

Answer: 12/13

Same triangle. tan θ?
  1. 5 ÷ 12.

Answer: 5/12
