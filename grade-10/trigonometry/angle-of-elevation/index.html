Angles of elevation and depression

Height = distance × tan(angle of elevation).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The angle of elevation is the angle you look up from the horizontal. The angle of depression is the angle you look down.

You stand 50 m from a tower and look up 35°. tan 35° ≈ 0.7, so h ≈ 50 × 0.7 = 35 m.

Add your eye height if the problem says your eyes are 1.5 m above the ground.

See three worked examples

40 m away, elevation 45°
  1. h = 40 × tan 45° = 40 × 1.

Answer: 40 m

60 m away, elevation 31°, tan 31° ≈ 0.6
  1. 60 × 0.6.

Answer: 36 m

From a 30 m cliff, a boat is at a 45° angle of depression. How far out?
  1. distance = 30 ÷ tan 45°.

Answer: 30 m
