Grade 10 Math: Linear systems

Solving two equations at once by elimination, substitution and graphing, plus standard form and finding a line from two points. Seven lessons.

  1. 1Solving systems by eliminationAdd or subtract the two equations so that one variable disappears.
  2. 2Solving systems by substitutionIf one equation says what y equals, put that into the other equation.
  3. 3Finding both variablesA system is only solved when you know both x and y.
  4. 4Word problems with two unknownsName the two unknowns, write two equations from the two facts, then solve.
  5. 5Standard form of a lineRearrange Ax + By = C to get y alone. The number in front of x is the slope.
  6. 6Point of intersectionTwo lines meet at one point, unless they are parallel or are the same line.
  7. 7Equation of a line through two pointsFind the slope first, then use one point to find b.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
