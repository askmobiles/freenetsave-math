Standard form of a line

Rearrange Ax + By = C to get y alone. The number in front of x is the slope.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A line can be written two ways:

  • Slope-intercept form: y = mx + b (slope m, y-intercept b).
  • Standard form: Ax + By = C (whole numbers, A usually positive).

Converting

2x + 3y = 12 → 3y = −2x + 12 → y = −(2/3)x + 4. Slope −2/3, y-intercept 4.

Shortcut: the slope of Ax + By = C is −A/B.

Intercepts are easy in standard form

Put x = 0: 3y = 12, y = 4. Put y = 0: 2x = 12, x = 6. Plot (0, 4) and (6, 0) and join them.

Divide every term by the number in front of y.

See three worked examples

Slope of 4x + 2y = 10
  1. 2y = −4x + 10.
  2. y = −2x + 5.

Answer: −2

Slope of 3x − y = 7
  1. −y = −3x + 7.
  2. y = 3x − 7.

Answer: 3

x-intercept of 5x + 4y = 20
  1. Put y = 0: 5x = 20.

Answer: 4
