Powers of products and quotients

When a whole product is raised to a power, every factor inside the brackets gets that power.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(2x³)² means (2x³)(2x³). Group the numbers and the x's: 2 × 2 × x³ × x³ = 4x⁶.

(ab)n = anbn and (xm)n = xmn

Steps

  1. Raise the number in front to the power.
  2. Multiply each variable's exponent by the power.
  3. Watch the sign: a negative number to an even power is positive, to an odd power is negative.

(−3a²b)³ = (−3)³ a⁶ b³ = −27a⁶b³.

Quotients

(x²/3)³ = x⁶/27. The top and the bottom both get the power.

Common mistake: (2x³)² is not 2x⁶. The 2 must be squared too.

See three worked examples

(5x⁴)²
  1. 5² = 25.
  2. (x⁴)² = x⁸.

Answer: 25x⁸

(−2a³b²)³
  1. (−2)³ = −8.
  2. a⁹ and b⁶.

Answer: −8a⁹b⁶

(3x²)² × 2x
  1. (3x²)² = 9x⁴.
  2. 9x⁴ × 2x = 18x⁵.

Answer: 18x⁵
