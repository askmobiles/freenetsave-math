Squaring a binomial

(x + a)² = x² + 2ax + a². The middle term is twice the product.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

(x + 4)² means (x + 4)(x + 4). The square splits into a big square, a small square and two equal rectangles.

(x + a)² = x² + 2ax + a² and (x − a)² = x² − 2ax + a²

Common mistake: (x + 4)² is NOT x² + 16. You must include the middle term 8x.

Spotting one

x² + 12x + 36 is a perfect square because 36 = 6² and 12 = 2 × 6. So it equals (x + 6)².

See three worked examples

(x + 7)²
  1. 2 × 7 = 14, 7² = 49.

Answer: x² + 14x + 49

(x − 5)²
  1. 2 × (−5) = −10, (−5)² = 25.

Answer: x² − 10x + 25

Factor x² − 18x + 81.
  1. 81 = 9², 18 = 2 × 9, minus sign.

Answer: (x − 9)²
