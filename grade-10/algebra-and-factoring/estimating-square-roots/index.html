Estimating square roots

Use the perfect squares on either side to trap a square root between two whole numbers.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

√50 is not a whole number. But 7² = 49 and 8² = 64, and 50 is between them, so √50 is between 7 and 8.

Getting closer

50 is very close to 49, so √50 is just a little over 7. A good estimate is 7.1. (A calculator gives 7.071.)

Perfect squares to know

1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144, 169, 196, 225.

Check: square your estimate. 7.1² = 50.41, which is close to 50. ✓

See three worked examples

√30 is between which whole numbers?
  1. 5² = 25 and 6² = 36.
  2. 30 is between 25 and 36.

Answer: 5 and 6

Estimate √85 to one decimal place.
  1. 9² = 81, 10² = 100.
  2. 85 is 4 of the 19 steps from 81, about 0.2 of the way.

Answer: about 9.2

A square room has area 70 m². About how long is each wall?
  1. √70: 8² = 64, 9² = 81.
  2. 70 is a bit under halfway.

Answer: about 8.4 m
