Vertex form and transformations

y = a(x − h)² + k has its vertex at (h, k).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Start with y = x². Changing the numbers moves and stretches it:

  • h: moves left or right. (x − 3)² moves 3 right; (x + 2)² moves 2 left.
  • k: moves up (+) or down (−).
  • a: if negative, it opens down. If bigger than 1, it is narrower (stretched); between 0 and 1, wider.

y = (x − 3)² + 2 (blue): vertex (3, 2), opens up. y = −2(x + 2)² + 1 (orange): vertex (−2, 1), opens down, narrower.

The h sign flips: (x + 2)² means h = −2.

See three worked examples

Vertex of y = (x − 5)² − 4
  1. h = 5, k = −4.

Answer: (5, −4)

Vertex of y = −3(x + 1)² + 6
  1. h = −1, k = 6.
  2. a = −3: opens down.

Answer: (−1, 6), maximum

Axis of symmetry of y = 2(x − 7)²
  1. h = 7.

Answer: x = 7
