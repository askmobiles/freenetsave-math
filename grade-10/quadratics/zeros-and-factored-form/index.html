Factored form: zeros and y-intercept

In y = a(x − r)(x − s), the zeros are r and s. Put x = 0 to get the y-intercept.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The factored form y = a(x − r)(x − s) shows where the graph crosses the x-axis: at x = r and x = s. These are the zeros.

y = (x − 1)(x + 3): zeros at x = 1 and x = −3 (change the signs!).

y-intercept

Put x = 0: (0 − 1)(0 + 3) = −3. So it crosses the y-axis at −3.

Sign flip: (x + 3) = 0 when x = −3.

See three worked examples

Zeros of y = (x − 4)(x + 2)
  1. x − 4 = 0, x + 2 = 0.

Answer: 4 and −2

y-intercept of y = 2(x − 3)(x − 5)
  1. 2(−3)(−5).

Answer: 30

Zeros of y = −(x + 1)(x + 6)
  1. x = −1, x = −6.

Answer: −1 and −6
