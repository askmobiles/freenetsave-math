Axis of symmetry

A parabola is a mirror image of itself across a vertical line through the vertex.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The axis of symmetry is the vertical line x = h through the vertex. The two halves of the parabola match exactly.

From the zeros

The axis is halfway between the zeros: x = (r + s) ÷ 2. Zeros −1 and 5 → axis x = 2.

Using symmetry

If (0, −2.5) is on the graph and the axis is x = 2, then (4, −2.5) is too — the same distance on the other side.

The vertex is always on the axis.

See three worked examples

Zeros at 3 and 9
  1. (3 + 9) ÷ 2.

Answer: x = 6

Zeros at −8 and 2
  1. (−8 + 2) ÷ 2.

Answer: x = −3

Axis x = 1 and (−2, 5) is on the graph. Find another point.
  1. −2 is 3 left of 1.
  2. 3 right of 1 is 4.

Answer: (4, 5)
