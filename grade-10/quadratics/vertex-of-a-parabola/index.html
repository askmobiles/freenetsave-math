The vertex of a parabola

The vertex is the turning point. Its x-coordinate is −b ÷ (2a).

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The graph of y = ax² + bx + c is a parabola. It turns around at the vertex.

  • a > 0: opens up, vertex is a minimum.
  • a < 0: opens down, vertex is a maximum.

Finding it

x = −b ÷ (2a)

y = x² − 4x + 1: x = 4 ÷ 2 = 2. Then y = 4 − 8 + 1 = −3. Vertex (2, −3).

Two steps: find x with the formula, then put it back in for y.

See three worked examples

Vertex of y = x² + 6x + 5
  1. x = −6 ÷ 2 = −3.
  2. y = 9 − 18 + 5 = −4.

Answer: (−3, −4)

Vertex of y = −2x² + 8x
  1. x = −8 ÷ (−4) = 2.
  2. y = −8 + 16 = 8.

Answer: (2, 8), a maximum

Does y = 3x² − 12x + 1 have a max or a min?
  1. a = 3 is positive.

Answer: a minimum
