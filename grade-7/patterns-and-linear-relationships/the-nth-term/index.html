Using the nth term rule

A rule like 2n + 1 finds any term. Put the term number in place of n.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Each picture adds 2 squares. The rule is 2n + 1, where n is the term number.

Term 3: 2 × 3 + 1 = 7 squares. Term 50: 2 × 50 + 1 = 101 squares, without drawing them all.

The steps

  1. Put the term number in place of n.
  2. Multiply first.
  3. Then add or subtract.

The number in front of n is the jump. In 4n − 2, each term is 4 more than the one before.

See three worked examples

Rule 2n + 8. Find term 19.
  1. 2 × 19 = 38.
  2. 38 + 8.

Answer: 46

Rule 4n − 2. Find term 16.
  1. 4 × 16 = 64.
  2. 64 − 2.

Answer: 62

Rule 7n + 2. Find term 21.
  1. 7 × 21 = 147.
  2. 147 + 2.

Answer: 149
