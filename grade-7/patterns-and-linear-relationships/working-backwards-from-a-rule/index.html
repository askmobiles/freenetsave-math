Working backwards from a rule

Start at the output and undo each step, last step first.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A machine multiplies by 4, then adds 6. The output is 10. What went in?

  1. The last step was + 6. Undo it: 10 − 6 = 4.
  2. The step before was × 4. Undo it: 4 ÷ 4 = 1.

Check: 1 × 4 + 6 = 10. ✓

Opposites: + becomes −, − becomes +, × becomes ÷, and ÷ becomes ×.

See three worked examples

Multiply by 4, then add 6. Output 10. Input?
  1. Undo + 6: 10 − 6 = 4.
  2. Undo × 4: 4 ÷ 4.

Answer: 1

Multiply by 4, then add 2. Output 26. Input?
  1. Undo + 2: 24.
  2. Undo × 4: 24 ÷ 4.

Answer: 6

Multiply by 3, then take away 5. Output 16. Input?
  1. Undo − 5: 16 + 5 = 21.
  2. Undo × 3: 21 ÷ 3.

Answer: 7
