Percent increase and decrease

Find how much it changed, then compare that change with where it started.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A price goes from $220 to $264.

The steps

  1. Change = new − old: 264 − 220 = 44.
  2. Divide by the old amount: 44 ÷ 220 = 0.2.
  3. Times 100: 20% increase.

If the number goes down, it is a decrease. From 80 to 60, the change is 20, and 20 ÷ 80 = 0.25, a 25% decrease.

Always divide by the starting amount, not the new one.

See three worked examples

A price goes from $220 to $264. Percent increase?
  1. Change: 264 − 220 = 44.
  2. 44 ÷ 220 = 0.2.

Answer: 20%

A price goes from $180 to $198. Percent increase?
  1. Change: 198 − 180 = 18.
  2. 18 ÷ 180 = 0.1.

Answer: 10%

A price goes from $80 to $60. Percent decrease?
  1. Change: 80 − 60 = 20.
  2. 20 ÷ 80 = 0.25.

Answer: 25% decrease
