Adding tips and tax

Find the percent of the bill, then add it on top.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A restaurant bill is $80. You want to leave a 15% tip.

10% of 80 is 8. 5% is half of that, 4. So 15% is 8 + 4 = $12, and the total is 80 + 12 = $92.

The steps

  1. Find the extra: bill × percent ÷ 100.
  2. Add the extra to the bill.

Or do it in one step: adding 15% is the same as multiplying by 1.15. 80 × 1.15 = 92.

Ontario tax is 13%. 10% plus 3%: for $140, that is 14 + 4.20 = $18.20 tax.

See three worked examples

A bill is $180. Add 15%.
  1. 10% = 18, 5% = 9, so 15% = 27.
  2. 180 + 27.

Answer: $207

A bill is $120. Add 10%.
  1. 10% = 12.
  2. 120 + 12.

Answer: $132

A bill is $140. Add 13% tax.
  1. 10% = 14, 3% = 4.20, so 13% = 18.20.
  2. 140 + 18.20.

Answer: $158.20
