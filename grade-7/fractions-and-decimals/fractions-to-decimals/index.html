Fractions to decimals

A fraction is a division. 3/8 means 3 ÷ 8, which is 0.375.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The fraction bar means "divided by". So to change any fraction to a decimal, divide the top by the bottom.

On the line, 3 eighths sits at 0.375. Every eighth is 0.125, and 3 × 0.125 = 0.375.

Worth knowing by heart

1/2 = 0.5  ·  1/4 = 0.25  ·  3/4 = 0.75  ·  1/5 = 0.2  ·  1/8 = 0.125  ·  1/10 = 0.1

Repeating decimals

Some never stop: 1/3 = 0.333… and 2/3 = 0.666… You can round them, for example 1/3 ≈ 0.33.

Shortcut: if the bottom goes into 10 or 100, make an equivalent fraction. 3/20 = 15/100 = 0.15.

See three worked examples

Write 1/2 as a decimal.
  1. 1 ÷ 2.

Answer: 0.5

Write 3/8 as a decimal.
  1. 3 ÷ 8: 8 goes into 30 three times (24), remainder 6.
  2. 60 ÷ 8 = 7 remainder 4, and 40 ÷ 8 = 5.

Answer: 0.375

Write 7/8 as a decimal.
  1. Each eighth is 0.125.
  2. 7 × 0.125.

Answer: 0.875
