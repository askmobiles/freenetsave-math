Subtracting fractions with different denominators

Same idea as adding: make the pieces the same size, then take away.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

How much is 3/4 − 1/3? Cut both into twelfths.

The steps

  1. Find a common denominator.
  2. Change both fractions so they have that denominator.
  3. Take away the tops. Keep the bottom. Simplify.

Whole numbers: 1 is the same as 4/4, 5/5 or 12/12. So 1 − 2/5 = 5/5 − 2/5 = 3/5.

See three worked examples

1/2 − 1/3
  1. Common denominator 6.
  2. 3/6 − 2/6.

Answer: 1/6

3/4 − 1/3
  1. Common denominator 12.
  2. 9/12 − 4/12.

Answer: 5/12

3/5 − 1/2
  1. Common denominator 10.
  2. 6/10 − 5/10.

Answer: 1/10
