Decimals to fractions

Read the decimal out loud. 0.75 is "75 hundredths", so it is 75/100, which simplifies to 3/4.

Learn

The last digit tells you the bottom number.

Decimal0.60.750.05
Say it6 tenths75 hundredths5 hundredths
Fraction6/1075/1005/100
Simplest3/53/41/20

The steps

  1. Write the digits over 10, 100 or 1,000, depending on the last place.
  2. Divide top and bottom by the same number until you can't go further.

For 0.75: 75/100. Both divide by 25, giving 3/4.

More than 1? 1.25 = 1 and 25/100 = 1 1/4, or 5/4 as an improper fraction.

See three worked examples

Write 0.6 as a fraction.
  1. 6 tenths: 6/10.
  2. Divide top and bottom by 2.

Answer: 3/5

Write 0.75 as a fraction.
  1. 75 hundredths: 75/100.
  2. Divide top and bottom by 25.

Answer: 3/4

Write 0.05 as a fraction.
  1. 5 hundredths: 5/100.
  2. Divide top and bottom by 5.

Answer: 1/20
