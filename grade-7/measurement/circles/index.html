Circles: area and circumference

Area uses the radius squared. The distance around uses the diameter. Both use π = 3.14.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two formulas

  • Area (space inside) = π × r × r.
  • Circumference (distance around) = π × d.

Radius 4: area = 3.14 × 4 × 4 = 50.24. Diameter 10: circumference = 3.14 × 10 = 31.4.

Which one? "Cover", "paint", "space inside" means area. "Around", "fence", "edge" means circumference.

See three worked examples

Circle with radius 4. Area?
  1. 4 × 4 = 16.
  2. 16 × 3.14.

Answer: 50.24

Circle with diameter 10. Circumference?
  1. 3.14 × 10.

Answer: 31.4

Circle with radius 10. Area?
  1. 10 × 10 = 100.
  2. 100 × 3.14.

Answer: 314
