Volume of a triangular prism

Find the area of the triangle at the end, then multiply by how long the prism is.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A triangular prism is a triangle pushed out along a length, like a tent or a chocolate bar box.

For base 9 cm, height 12 cm and length 6 cm: triangle area = 9 × 12 ÷ 2 = 54, and volume = 54 × 6 = 324 cm³.

Two different heights. The triangle's height is on the end face. The length is how far the prism goes back. Don't mix them up.

See three worked examples

Triangle base 9, height 12; length 6.
  1. 9 × 12 ÷ 2 = 54.
  2. 54 × 6.

Answer: 324 cm³

Triangle base 10, height 10; length 4.
  1. 10 × 10 ÷ 2 = 50.
  2. 50 × 4.

Answer: 200 cm³

Triangle base 2, height 6; length 5.
  1. 2 × 6 ÷ 2 = 6.
  2. 6 × 5.

Answer: 30 cm³
