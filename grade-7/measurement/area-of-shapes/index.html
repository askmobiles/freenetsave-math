Area of rectangles and triangles

A rectangle is length × width. A triangle is half of a rectangle, so it is base × height ÷ 2.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Both shapes below have the same base and height. The triangle fits exactly into half the rectangle.

  • Rectangle: length × width.
  • Triangle: base × height ÷ 2.

The height of a triangle is measured straight up from the base, at a square corner, not along a slanted side.

Units: area is in square units, such as cm² or m².

See three worked examples

Rectangle 5 by 19. Area?
  1. 5 × 19.

Answer: 95

Triangle with base 17 and height 6. Area?
  1. 17 × 6 = 102.
  2. 102 ÷ 2.

Answer: 51

Triangle with base 8 and height 5. Area?
  1. 8 × 5 = 40.
  2. 40 ÷ 2.

Answer: 20
