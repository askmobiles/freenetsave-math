Volume of a cylinder (in terms of π)

Square the radius, multiply by the height, and keep π as a letter at the end.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A cylinder is a stack of circles. Its volume is the area of the circle (πr²) times the height (h).

Leaving π in the answer

Instead of multiplying by 3.14, you can keep π as a symbol. For radius 5 and height 9: 5 × 5 × 9 = 225, so the volume is 225π.

This is exact. If you need a number, multiply at the end: 225 × 3.14 = 706.5.

Square only the radius. 5² × 9 is 25 × 9, not (5 × 9)².

See three worked examples

Radius 5, height 9.
  1. 5 × 5 = 25.
  2. 25 × 9 = 225.

Answer: 225π

Radius 7, height 2.
  1. 7 × 7 = 49.
  2. 49 × 2 = 98.

Answer: 98π

Radius 3, height 10.
  1. 3 × 3 = 9.
  2. 9 × 10 = 90.

Answer: 90π
