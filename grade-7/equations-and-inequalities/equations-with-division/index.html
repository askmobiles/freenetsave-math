Equations with division

Dividing is undone by multiplying. Undo the plus or minus first, then multiply both sides.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Solve x ÷ 4 + 2 = 5.

Sometimes it is written as a fraction: x/4 + 2 = 5 means the same thing.

Check: 12 ÷ 4 + 2 = 3 + 2 = 5. ✓

See three worked examples

x ÷ 3 + 2 = 11
  1. Take 2: x ÷ 3 = 9.
  2. Multiply by 3.

Answer: x = 27

x ÷ 8 + 6 = 10
  1. Take 6: x ÷ 8 = 4.
  2. Multiply by 8.

Answer: x = 32

x ÷ 4 − 1 = 5
  1. Add 1: x ÷ 4 = 6.
  2. Multiply by 4.

Answer: x = 24
