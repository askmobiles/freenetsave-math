One-step equations

An equation is a balance. Do the opposite operation to both sides to get x alone.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

x + 3 = 10 means the two sides weigh the same.

Take 3 away from both sides and it stays balanced: x = 10 − 3 = 7.

Undo with the opposite

  • + is undone by −, and − by +.
  • × is undone by ÷, and ÷ by ×.

4x = 36 means 4 times x. Divide both sides by 4: x = 9.

Check it. Put your answer back in: 7 + 3 = 10. ✓

See three worked examples

x + 3 = −9
  1. Take 3 from both sides.
  2. −9 − 3.

Answer: x = −12

x + 9 = 17
  1. Take 9 from both sides.
  2. 17 − 9.

Answer: x = 8

9x = −108
  1. Divide both sides by 9.
  2. −108 ÷ 9.

Answer: x = −12
