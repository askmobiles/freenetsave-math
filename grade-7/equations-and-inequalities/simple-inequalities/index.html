Simple inequalities

Solve it like an equation, but the answer is a range of numbers, not just one.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

x + 3 > 12 means "x plus 3 is greater than 12". Take 3 from both sides: x > 9.

The open circle means 9 itself does not work (9 + 3 is 12, not more than 12). Every number to the right does. The smallest whole number that works is 10.

The symbols

  • > greater than   < less than
  • ≥ greater than or equal to   ≤ less than or equal to

Not a whole number? 2x > 15 gives x > 7.5. The smallest whole number above 7.5 is 8.

See three worked examples

x + 3 > 12. Smallest whole number?
  1. Take 3: x > 9.
  2. 9 does not work, so the next one up.

Answer: 10

x + 10 > 23. Smallest whole number?
  1. Take 10: x > 13.

Answer: 14

2x > 15. Smallest whole number?
  1. Divide by 2: x > 7.5.
  2. The next whole number after 7.5.

Answer: 8
