Better buy: price for one

Divide the pack price by how many are in it. The lower price for one is the better buy.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Which is the better deal for granola bars?

PriceCountPrice for one
Pack A$1.804$0.45
Pack B$4.808$0.60

Pack A: 1.80 ÷ 4 = $0.45 each. Pack B: 4.80 ÷ 8 = $0.60 each. Pack A is the better buy, even though Pack B has more.

Bigger isn't always cheaper. Always compare the price for one, or for one litre or one kilogram.

See three worked examples

A: 4 for $1.80. B: 8 for $4.80. Lower price for one?
  1. A: 1.80 ÷ 4 = 0.45.
  2. B: 4.80 ÷ 8 = 0.60.

Answer: $0.45

A: 3 for $3.45. B: 6 for $3.90. Lower price for one?
  1. A: 3.45 ÷ 3 = 1.15.
  2. B: 3.90 ÷ 6 = 0.65.

Answer: $0.65

A: 5 for $2.25. B: 6 for $3.30. Lower price for one?
  1. A: 2.25 ÷ 5 = 0.45.
  2. B: 3.30 ÷ 6 = 0.55.

Answer: $0.45
