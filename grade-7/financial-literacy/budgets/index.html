Budgets

Income minus spending is what you have left to save.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Arjun gets $200 a month from a weekend job.

  1. Add the spending: 20 + 30 + 10 = $60.
  2. Take it away: 200 − 60 = $140 left over.

Plan saving first. Many people save a set amount before spending, so the saving always happens.

See three worked examples

Income $200. Spending $20, $30, $10.
  1. Spending: 60.
  2. 200 − 60.

Answer: $140

Income $425. Spending $60, $20, $15.
  1. Spending: 95.
  2. 425 − 95.

Answer: $330

Income $500. Spending $60, $10, $5.
  1. Spending: 75.
  2. 500 − 75.

Answer: $425
