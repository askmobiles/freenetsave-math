Speed, distance and time

Distance = speed × time. Cover the one you want in the triangle to see how to find it.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A car goes at 60 km/h. That means it covers 60 km every hour. In 3 hours it goes 60 × 3 = 180 km.

  • Want the distance? Multiply speed × time.
  • Want the speed? Divide distance ÷ time.
  • Want the time? Divide distance ÷ speed.

Watch the units. 30 minutes is 0.5 hours. At 6 km/h for 30 minutes you walk 6 × 0.5 = 3 km.

See three worked examples

A train goes 420 km in 6 hours. Speed?
  1. Speed = distance ÷ time.
  2. 420 ÷ 6.

Answer: 70 km/h

A car goes 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. Distance?
  1. Distance = speed × time.
  2. 60 × 2.5.

Answer: 150 km

A bus goes 240 km at 80 km/h. Time?
  1. Time = distance ÷ speed.
  2. 240 ÷ 80.

Answer: 3 hours
