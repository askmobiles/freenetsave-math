Solving proportions

Two equal fractions make a proportion. Whatever you do to the bottom, do the same to the top.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Solve 3/4 = x/20. The bottom went from 4 to 20, which is × 5. So the top goes from 3 to 3 × 5 = 15.

The double number line shows it: every 4 on the top line matches 3 on the bottom line.

The steps

  1. See what the known bottom was multiplied (or divided) by.
  2. Do the same to the top.

Real life: recipes, maps and prices are all proportions. If 2 cups make 12 cookies, 5 cups make 30 cookies.

See three worked examples

4/1 = x/2
  1. The bottom went from 1 to 2: × 2.
  2. 4 × 2.

Answer: x = 8

5/3 = x/15
  1. The bottom went from 3 to 15: × 5.
  2. 5 × 5.

Answer: x = 25

3/4 = x/20
  1. The bottom went from 4 to 20: × 5.
  2. 3 × 5.

Answer: x = 15
