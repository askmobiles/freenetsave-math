Median and mode

Sort first. The median is the middle; the mode is the one that appears most.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Take 20, 6, 2, 11, 6. Put them in order:

  • Median = the middle number = 6.
  • Mode = the most common = 6 (it appears twice).

With an even count, the median is halfway between the two middle numbers. For 1, 4, 7, 9 it is (4 + 7) ÷ 2 = 5.5.

No mode, or two modes: if every number appears once there is no mode. If two numbers tie for most, both are modes.

See three worked examples

20, 6, 2, 11, 6: median?
  1. In order: 2, 6, 6, 11, 20.
  2. The middle one.

Answer: 6

8, 19, 13, 18, 8: median?
  1. In order: 8, 8, 13, 18, 19.
  2. The middle one.

Answer: 13

4, 9, 1, 7: median?
  1. In order: 1, 4, 7, 9.
  2. (4 + 7) ÷ 2.

Answer: 5.5
