The mean

Add them all up and share equally: total ÷ how many.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The mean is what each would get if everything were shared out equally.

21 + 1 + 12 + 9 + 7 = 50. There are 5 numbers. 50 ÷ 5 = 10.

With negative numbers

Add carefully: 23 + (−1) + 14 + 8 = 44, and 44 ÷ 4 = 11.

Total = mean × count. Use this to find a missing number.

See three worked examples

Mean of 21, 1, 12, 9, 7
  1. Total 50.
  2. 50 ÷ 5.

Answer: 10

Mean of 7, 8, 10, 17, 1, 5
  1. Total 48.
  2. 48 ÷ 6.

Answer: 8

Mean of 23, −1, 14, 8
  1. Total 44.
  2. 44 ÷ 4.

Answer: 11
