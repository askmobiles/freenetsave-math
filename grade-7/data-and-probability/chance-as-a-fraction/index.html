Chance as a fraction

Chance = how many you want ÷ how many there are in all.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

You pick one marble without looking.

There is 1 red out of 7 marbles, so the chance of red is 1/7. The chance of blue is 6/7.

The steps

  1. Count the ones you want.
  2. Count all of them.
  3. Write wanted over total. Simplify if you can.

Check: the chances of every colour add up to 1. Here 1/7 + 6/7 = 7/7 = 1.

See three worked examples

1 red and 6 blue. Chance of red?
  1. Red: 1. Total: 7.

Answer: 1/7

7 red and 1 blue. Chance of red?
  1. Red: 7. Total: 8.

Answer: 7/8

3 red and 9 blue. Chance of red?
  1. Red: 3. Total: 12.
  2. 3/12 simplifies.

Answer: 1/4
