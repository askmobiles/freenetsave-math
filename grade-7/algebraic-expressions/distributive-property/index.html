Expanding brackets

Multiply the outside number by everything inside. 3(2x + 5) becomes 6x + 15.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Think of 3(2x + 5) as a rectangle 3 tall and (2x + 5) wide. Split it into two parts.

The two areas are 3 × 2x = 6x and 3 × 5 = 15. So 3(2x + 5) = 6x + 15.

With a minus

5(x − 3) = 5 × x − 5 × 3 = 5x − 15. Keep the minus sign.

Don't stop halfway. 3(2x + 5) is not 6x + 5. The 5 gets multiplied too.

See three worked examples

3(2x + 5)
  1. 3 × 2x = 6x.
  2. 3 × 5 = 15.

Answer: 6x + 15

2(4x + 4)
  1. 2 × 4x = 8x.
  2. 2 × 4 = 8.

Answer: 8x + 8

5(x − 3)
  1. 5 × x = 5x.
  2. 5 × 3 = 15, and keep the minus.

Answer: 5x − 15
