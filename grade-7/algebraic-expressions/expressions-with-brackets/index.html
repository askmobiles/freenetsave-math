Working out expressions with brackets

Put the number in place of the letter, work out the brackets, then multiply by the number outside.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Find the value of 3(x + 2) when x = 4.

A number written right next to brackets means multiply. 3(6) is 3 × 6.

Brackets first. 3(4 + 2) is 3 × 6 = 18, not 3 × 4 + 2 = 14.

See three worked examples

4(x + 6) when x = 7
  1. 4(7 + 6) = 4 × 13.

Answer: 52

5(x + 2) when x = 3
  1. 5(3 + 2) = 5 × 5.

Answer: 25

2(x − 3) when x = 10
  1. 2(10 − 3) = 2 × 7.

Answer: 14
