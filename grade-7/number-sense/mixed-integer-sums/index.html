Adding and subtracting integers

Adding a negative moves left on the number line. Taking away a negative moves right.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Work out −7 + 14 − (−8) one step at a time, from left to right.

  1. Start at −7 and add 14: move 14 right to 7.
  2. Take away −8. Taking away a negative is the same as adding: move 8 right to 15.

The rules

  • + (positive): move right.   + (−3) is the same as − 3: move left.
  • − (positive): move left.   − (−3) is the same as + 3: move right.

Two signs touching? Same signs make +. Different signs make −. So − (−8) becomes + 8 and + (−5) becomes − 5.

See three worked examples

−7 + 14 − (−8)
  1. −7 + 14 = 7.
  2. 7 − (−8) = 7 + 8.

Answer: 15

−4 + 1 − (−8)
  1. −4 + 1 = −3.
  2. −3 − (−8) = −3 + 8.

Answer: 5

−3 + (−5) − (−11)
  1. −3 + (−5) = −3 − 5 = −8.
  2. −8 − (−11) = −8 + 11.

Answer: 3
