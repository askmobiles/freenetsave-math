Multiplying and dividing integers

Multiply or divide the numbers as usual, then decide the sign: same signs give a positive, different signs give a negative.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Do the numbers first, then use these four rules for the sign.

Why is −3 × 4 negative? It means four lots of −3: −3 − 3 − 3 − 3 = −12.

Dividing follows the same rules: −20 ÷ 4 = −5 and −18 ÷ (−3) = 6.

Many negatives? Count them. An even number of negative signs gives a positive answer; an odd number gives a negative.

See three worked examples

−6 × 4
  1. 6 × 4 = 24.
  2. Different signs, so negative.

Answer: −24

−11 × (−5)
  1. 11 × 5 = 55.
  2. Same signs, so positive.

Answer: 55

−36 ÷ 9
  1. 36 ÷ 9 = 4.
  2. Different signs, so negative.

Answer: −4
