Perfect squares

A square number is a number times itself. You can arrange that many dots or tiles into a perfect square.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

5² ("5 squared") means 5 × 5. Lay out 25 tiles and they make a square 5 tiles wide and 5 tiles tall.

Squares worth knowing

1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144, 169, 196, 225

These are 1² up to 15². Knowing them makes square roots, area and the Pythagorean theorem much faster.

Not the same as doubling. 5² is 5 × 5 = 25, not 5 × 2 = 10.

See three worked examples

8²
  1. 8 × 8.

Answer: 64

12²
  1. 12 × 12 = 12 × 10 + 12 × 2 = 120 + 24.

Answer: 144

15²
  1. 15 × 15 = 15 × 10 + 15 × 5 = 150 + 75.

Answer: 225
