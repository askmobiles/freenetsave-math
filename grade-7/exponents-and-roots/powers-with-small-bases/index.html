Powers and exponents

The small raised number, the exponent, tells you how many times to multiply the base by itself.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

In 3⁴, 3 is the base and 4 is the exponent. It means four 3s multiplied together.

  • 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8
  • 10³ = 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000 (three zeros)
  • Any number to the power 1 is itself: 7¹ = 7.

Powers grow fast. 2⁵ = 32 but 2¹⁰ = 1,024. Each step up doubles the answer.

See three worked examples

2³
  1. 2 × 2 = 4.
  2. 4 × 2.

Answer: 8

3⁴
  1. 3 × 3 = 9.
  2. 9 × 3 = 27.
  3. 27 × 3.

Answer: 81

5⁴
  1. 5 × 5 = 25.
  2. 25 × 5 = 125.
  3. 125 × 5.

Answer: 625
