Coordinates in four quadrants

A point (x, y) goes across first, then up or down. The signs tell you which quarter of the grid it is in.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The two axes cut the grid into four quadrants, numbered 1 to 4 going around from the top right.

  • Quadrant 1: x positive, y positive (+, +)
  • Quadrant 2: x negative, y positive (−, +)
  • Quadrant 3: both negative (−, −)
  • Quadrant 4: x positive, y negative (+, −)

On an axis? A point with a 0, like (0, 5) or (4, 0), sits on an axis and is not in any quadrant. (0, 0) is the origin.

See three worked examples

(−6, −5): which quadrant?
  1. x is negative, y is negative.

Answer: 3

(6, −7): which quadrant?
  1. x is positive, y is negative.

Answer: 4

(−2, 4): which quadrant?
  1. x is negative, y is positive.

Answer: 2
