Angle pairs: 90° and 180°

Angles in a square corner add to 90°. Angles on a straight line add to 180°. Take away the one you know.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Two angles that fill a square corner add up to 90°. They are called complementary.

Two angles that sit on a straight line add up to 180°. They are called supplementary.

Look for the little square. It marks a 90° corner. A straight line with no square means 180°.

See three worked examples

Two angles add to 180°. One is 66°. The other?
  1. 180 − 66.

Answer: 114°

Two angles add to 180°. One is 102°. The other?
  1. 180 − 102.

Answer: 78°

Two angles add to 90°. One is 40°. The other?
  1. 90 − 40.

Answer: 50°
