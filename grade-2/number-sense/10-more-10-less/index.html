10 more, 10 less

Jumping by tens. Grade 2 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. 10 more makes the tens digit go up by one.
  2. 10 less makes the tens digit go down by one.
  3. The ones digit stays the same.

Read the worked examples next. Then try the practice questions, which show the steps after every answer.
