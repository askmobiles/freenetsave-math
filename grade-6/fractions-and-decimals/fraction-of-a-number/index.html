Fraction of a number

Like 3/4 of 20. Grade 6 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. Divide the number by the bottom of the fraction.
  2. Multiply the result by the top.
  3. Example: 3/4 of 20 is 20 ÷ 4 = 5, then 5 × 3 = 15.

Read the worked examples next. Then try the practice questions, which show the steps after every answer.

See worked examples

1/2 of 10

Divide by the bottom: 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Multiply by the top: 5 × 1 = 5.

Answer: 5

4/5 of 10

Divide by the bottom: 10 ÷ 5 = 2. Multiply by the top: 2 × 4 = 8.

Answer: 8

1/2 of 24

Divide by the bottom: 24 ÷ 2 = 12. Multiply by the top: 12 × 1 = 12.

Answer: 12
