Percent problems: part, whole and percent

Every percent problem has three numbers: the part, the whole and the percent. Give any two and you can find the third.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

In "30% of 250 is 75", the whole is 250, the percent is 30% and the part is 75.

One rule connects them: part = percent × whole. Write the percent as a decimal first, so 30% becomes 0.30.

  • Find the part: multiply. 30% of 250 = 0.30 × 250 = 75.
  • Find the whole: divide the part by the percent. If 75% of a number is 240, the number is 240 ÷ 0.75 = 320.
  • Find the percent: divide the part by the whole, then multiply by 100. 18 out of 72 is 18 ÷ 72 = 0.25 = 25%.

The bar shows "75% of a number is 240". The shaded part is 240. The whole bar, 100%, must be more than 240. That tells you to divide, not multiply.

Quick check: the part is smaller than the whole when the percent is under 100. If your "whole" came out smaller than the part, you multiplied when you should have divided.

See three worked examples

75% of a number is 240. What is the number?
  1. You know the part (240) and the percent (75% = 0.75). You need the whole.
  2. Divide the part by the percent: 240 ÷ 0.75.
  3. 240 ÷ 0.75 = 320. Check: 0.75 × 320 = 240.

Answer: 320

18 is what percent of 72?
  1. You know the part (18) and the whole (72). You need the percent.
  2. Divide: 18 ÷ 72 = 0.25.
  3. Multiply by 100.

Answer: 25%

What is 30% of 250?
  1. You know the percent and the whole. You need the part.
  2. Write 30% as 0.30 and multiply: 0.30 × 250.
  3. 0.30 × 250 = 75.

Answer: 75
