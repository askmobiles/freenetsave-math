Collecting like terms

Like terms have exactly the same letter part. Add them together to make an expression shorter.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Algebra tiles make it easy to see. A long blue tile is x. A small orange tile is 1.

Put the x tiles together and the 1 tiles together: 5 x tiles and 6 ones. So 3x + 2 + 2x + 4 = 5x + 6.

What counts as "like"?

  • 3x and 7x are like terms: both are x.
  • 4 and 9 are like terms: both are plain numbers.
  • x and x² are not like terms. Neither are x and y.

Keep the sign with the term

In 7a − 3 − 2a + 8, the terms are 7a, −3, −2a and +8. So 7a − 2a = 5a and −3 + 8 = 5. The answer is 5a + 5.

You cannot join x terms and numbers. 5x + 6 is as short as it gets. It is not 11x.

See three worked examples

Simplify 3x + 2 + 2x + 4.
  1. x terms: 3x + 2x = 5x.
  2. Plain numbers: 2 + 4 = 6.

Answer: 5x + 6

Simplify 7a − 3 − 2a + 8.
  1. a terms: 7a − 2a = 5a.
  2. Plain numbers: −3 + 8 = 5.

Answer: 5a + 5

Simplify 4x² + 3x − x² + 2x.
  1. x² terms: 4x² − x² = 3x².
  2. x terms: 3x + 2x = 5x.
  3. x² and x are not like terms, so stop.

Answer: 3x² + 5x
