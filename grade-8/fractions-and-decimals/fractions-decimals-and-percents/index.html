Fractions, decimals and percents

A fraction, a decimal and a percent can all name the same amount. This lesson shows how to move from one form to another.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Percent means "out of 100". So 75% is 75 out of 100, which is the fraction 75/100 and the decimal 0.75.

All three bars show the same amount. Only the name changes.

Percent to decimal, and back

  • Percent to decimal: divide by 100. The decimal point moves two places to the left. 8% = 0.08 and 135% = 1.35.
  • Decimal to percent: multiply by 100. The point moves two places to the right. 0.4 = 40% and 0.375 = 37.5%.

Fraction to decimal

Divide the top by the bottom. 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375. Then multiply by 100 if you want a percent: 37.5%.

Percents over 100

A percent can be more than 100. 100% is one whole, so 135% is one whole and 35 hundredths more, or 1.35.

Worth knowing by heart: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%, 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%, 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%.

See three worked examples

Write 135% as a decimal.
  1. Percent means out of 100, so divide by 100.
  2. Move the decimal point two places to the left: 135. becomes 1.35.

Answer: 1.35

Write 0.08 as a percent.
  1. Multiply by 100.
  2. Move the decimal point two places to the right: 0.08 becomes 8.

Answer: 8%

Write 3/8 as a percent.
  1. Divide the top by the bottom: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.
  2. Multiply by 100: 0.375 becomes 37.5.

Answer: 37.5%
