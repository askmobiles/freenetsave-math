Unit rates

A unit rate tells you how much for one: one hour, one item, one kilogram. It makes rates easy to compare.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A rate compares two different kinds of amount, such as kilometres and hours. A unit rate is the rate for exactly 1 of the second amount.

To find it, divide the first amount by the second.

A car goes 240 km in 3 hours. In 1 hour it goes 240 ÷ 3 = 80 km. Its speed is 80 km per hour, written 80 km/h.

  • Speed: distance ÷ time, in km/h.
  • Price for one: cost ÷ number of items.
  • Typing speed: words ÷ minutes.

Read the "per". "km per hour" means km ÷ hours. "Minutes per km" means minutes ÷ km. They are different rates for the same trip.

See three worked examples

A car travels 100 km in 2 hours. What is its speed in km per hour?
  1. Speed is distance ÷ time.
  2. 100 ÷ 2 = 50.

Answer: 50 km/h

A car travels 240 km in 3 hours. What is its speed?
  1. 240 ÷ 3 = 80.

Answer: 80 km/h

Maya types 450 words in 6 minutes. How many words per minute is that?
  1. Words per minute is words ÷ minutes.
  2. 450 ÷ 6 = 75.

Answer: 75 words per minute
