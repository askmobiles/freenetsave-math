Price for one. Grade 8 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. You know the price for several kilograms.
  2. Divide the price by the number of kilograms.
  3. That is the price for one.

Read the worked examples next. Then try the practice questions, which show the steps after every answer.

See worked examples

8 kg of rice costs $59.2.Dollars for 1 kg?

59.2 ÷ 8 = 7.4.

Answer: 7.4

10 kg of rice costs $15.Dollars for 1 kg?

15 ÷ 10 = 1.5.

Answer: 1.5

11 kg of rice costs $86.9.Dollars for 1 kg?

86.9 ÷ 11 = 7.9.

Answer: 7.9
