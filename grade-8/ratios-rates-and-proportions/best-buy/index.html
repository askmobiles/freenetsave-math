Finding the best buy

A bigger package is not always the better deal. Compare the price of one unit to know for sure.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The unit price is the price for one item, one kilogram, one litre or one of whatever is being sold. Find it by dividing the price by the amount.

PackagePricePrice for 100 g
500 g$4.00$4.00 ÷ 5 = $0.80
750 g$5.70$5.70 ÷ 7.5 = $0.76

The larger package costs more, but each 100 g costs less, so it is the better buy.

  1. Pick a unit that both items can use, such as 1 item or 100 g.
  2. Divide each price by how many units it holds.
  3. The lower unit price is the better buy.

Cheaper per unit is not always better for you. If you will throw half of the big package away, the small one may cost you less.

See three worked examples

9 kg of rice costs $25.20. What is the price for 1 kg?
  1. Divide the price by the number of kilograms.
  2. 25.20 ÷ 9 = 2.80.

Answer: $2.80 per kg

Which is the better buy: 500 g for $4.00 or 750 g for $5.70?
  1. Use 100 g as the unit. 500 g is 5 lots of 100 g; 750 g is 7.5 lots.
  2. $4.00 ÷ 5 = $0.80. $5.70 ÷ 7.5 = $0.76.
  3. $0.76 is less.

Answer: 750 g for $5.70

Which is the better buy: 3 for $5 or 5 for $8?
  1. 3 for $5: 5 ÷ 3 = about $1.67 each.
  2. 5 for $8: 8 ÷ 5 = $1.60 each.

Answer: 5 for $8
