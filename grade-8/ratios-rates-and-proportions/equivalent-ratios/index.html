Ratios and equivalent ratios

A ratio compares two amounts. Equivalent ratios compare them in the same way, using different numbers.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A ratio such as 2 : 3 compares two amounts. "The ratio of flour to milk is 2 : 3" means that for every 2 cups of flour there are 3 cups of milk.

Each box is one equal part. If each part is 4 cups, the recipe uses 8 cups of flour and 12 cups of milk. So 2 : 3 and 8 : 12 are equivalent ratios: they describe the same mix.

Making equivalent ratios

Multiply or divide both numbers by the same number. 3 : 5 = 6 : 10 = 12 : 20.

Simplest form

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. 18 : 24 has a common factor of 6, so it simplifies to 3 : 4.

Order matters. Boys to girls 3 : 4 is not the same as girls to boys 3 : 4. Always keep the amounts in the order the question gives.

See three worked examples

3 : 5 = 12 : ?
  1. Look at the first numbers: 3 became 12, so it was multiplied by 4.
  2. Do the same to the second number: 5 × 4 = 20.

Answer: 20

Write 18 : 24 in simplest form.
  1. The greatest common factor of 18 and 24 is 6.
  2. Divide both: 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4.

Answer: 3 : 4

A recipe uses 2 cups of flour for every 3 cups of milk. How much milk goes with 8 cups of flour?
  1. Flour went from 2 to 8, which is × 4.
  2. Milk must also be × 4: 3 × 4 = 12.

Answer: 12 cups
