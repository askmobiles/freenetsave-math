Budgets

A budget is a plan for your money. Income comes in; spending and saving go out.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Maya earns $1,300 a month from a part-time job. Here is her plan.

The steps

  1. Add up everything that is spent.
  2. Take the spending away from the income.
  3. What is left can be saved.

Maya spends 75 + 100 + 300 + 200 = $675, so she can save 1,300 − 675 = $625.

Many people save a percent first. Saving 20% of $1,300 is 0.2 × 1,300 = $260.

Monthly or yearly? A $45 phone plan each month is 45 × 12 = $540 a year.

See three worked examples

Income $1,300. Spending $300, $75 and $100. How much is left?
  1. Spending: 300 + 75 + 100 = 475.
  2. 1,300 − 475.

Answer: $825

Income $1,450. Spending $225, $75 and $50. How much is left?
  1. Spending: 225 + 75 + 50 = 350.
  2. 1,450 − 350.

Answer: $1,100

Income $2,000. You save 20%. How much do you save?
  1. 20% = 0.2.
  2. 0.2 × 2,000.

Answer: $400
