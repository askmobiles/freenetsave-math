Sales tax (13% HST)

In Ontario most things have 13% tax added at the till. Multiply by 1.13 to get the total in one step.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

A hoodie has a price tag of $40. At the till, 13% HST is added.

Hoodie$40.00
HST 13%$5.20
Total$45.20

Two ways

  1. Tax, then add: 13% of 40 is 0.13 × 40 = 5.20. Then 40 + 5.20 = $45.20.
  2. One step: 40 × 1.13 = $45.20. The 1 keeps the price and the 0.13 adds the tax.

Before tax

If the total was $56.50, divide by 1.13: 56.50 ÷ 1.13 = $50 before tax.

Round money to the cent. $24.99 × 1.13 = 28.2387, which is $28.24.

See three worked examples

An item costs $70 before 13% tax. What is the total?
  1. Tax: 70 × 0.13 = 9.10.
  2. 70 + 9.10.

Answer: $79.10

An item costs $60 before tax. What is the total?
  1. One step: 60 × 1.13.

Answer: $67.80

A game costs $24.99 before tax. What is the total?
  1. 24.99 × 1.13 = 28.2387.
  2. Round to the cent.

Answer: $28.24
