Saving for a goal

Divide what you still need by what you save each week. If it does not divide evenly, round up.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

You want a bike that costs $360. You already have $60, and you can save $25 a week.

The steps

  1. Find what you still need: 360 − 60 = $300.
  2. Divide by what you save each week: 300 ÷ 25 = 12 weeks.

Round up

To save $250 at $30 a week: 250 ÷ 30 = 8.33… After 8 weeks you have only $240, so you need 9 weeks.

Working the other way: to save $150 in 10 weeks, save 150 ÷ 10 = $15 a week.

See three worked examples

  1. 540 ÷ 30.

Answer: 18 weeks
