Straight-line rules: y = mx + b

A rule like y = 2x + 1 turns any x into a y. Plot the points and they make a straight line.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The rule y = 2x + 1 says: take x, double it, then add 1. When x = 3, y = 2 × 3 + 1 = 7.

What m and b mean

  • m is the slope: how much y goes up each time x goes up by 1. Here m = 2, so each step right goes 2 up.
  • b is where the line crosses the y-axis, when x = 0. Here b = 1.

If m is negative, the line goes down from left to right. y = −3x + 4 drops 3 for every step right.

Multiply first, then add. In y = 2x + 1 with x = 3, do 2 × 3 = 6 first, then 6 + 1 = 7.

See three worked examples

y = 2x + 1. Find y when x = 3.
  1. Multiply: 2 × 3 = 6.
  2. Add 1.

Answer: y = 7

y = −3x + 4. Find y when x = 2.
  1. Multiply: −3 × 2 = −6.
  2. Add 4: −6 + 4.

Answer: y = −2

y = x/2 − 1. Find y when x = 8.
  1. Divide: 8 ÷ 2 = 4.
  2. Take away 1.

Answer: y = 3
