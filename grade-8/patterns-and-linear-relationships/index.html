Grade 8 Math: Patterns & linear relationships

Straight-line rules, slope, number patterns and tables of values, with graphs to see how they connect. Four lessons, each with an explanation, worked examples, practice, a matching game, a test and challenge problems.

  1. 1Straight-line rules: y = mx + bA rule like y = 2x + 1 turns any x into a y. Plot the points and they make a straight line.
  2. 2Slope: rise over runSlope tells you how steep a line is: how far it goes up (rise) for how far it goes across (run).
  3. 3Number patterns and the nth termFind the jump between terms and you can write a rule. The rule finds any term, even the 100th, without counting.
  4. 4Tables of valuesA table of values lists x and y pairs from a rule. Plot them and you get the graph of the rule.

How each lesson works

  1. Learn: a short explanation with a picture.
  2. See: three worked examples, and more whenever you ask.
  3. Try: 10 practice questions that show the steps.
  4. Play: a quick matching game.
  5. Test: 10 questions. Score 8 or more to complete the lesson.
  6. Challenge: harder problems for when the basics feel easy.
