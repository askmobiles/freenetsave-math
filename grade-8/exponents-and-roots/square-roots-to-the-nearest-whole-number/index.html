Estimating square roots to the nearest whole number

Once you know which two whole numbers a root is between, decide which one it is closer to.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

√50 is between 7 and 8. Which is it closer to? 50 is only 1 more than 49 but 14 less than 64, so √50 is much closer to 7.

The halfway check

The point halfway between 7 and 8 is 7.5, and 7.5² = 56.25. So:

  • numbers below 56.25 have roots that round to 7;
  • numbers above 56.25 have roots that round to 8.

This matters for numbers near the middle. 56 is closer to 49 by 7 and to 64 by 8, and √56 does round to 7. But √57 rounds to 8, even though 57 is closer to 64 by only 7 and to 49 by 8. The halfway square settles it.

Quick rule: the halfway point between n² and (n + 1)² is n² + n + 0.25. For n = 7 that is 49 + 7 + 0.25 = 56.25.

See three worked examples

Estimate √50 to the nearest whole number.
  1. √50 is between 7 and 8.
  2. The halfway square is 7.5² = 56.25. 50 is less than that.

Answer: 7

Estimate √120 to the nearest whole number.
  1. √120 is between 10 and 11, because 100 < 120 < 121.
  2. The halfway square is 10.5² = 110.25. 120 is more than that.

Answer: 11

Estimate √57 to the nearest whole number.
  1. √57 is between 7 and 8.
  2. The halfway square is 56.25. 57 is just above it.

Answer: 8
