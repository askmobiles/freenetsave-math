Cube roots

A cube root asks: what number, used three times as a factor, gives this?

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

3 × 3 × 3 = 27, so the cube root of 27 is 3. We write ∛27 = 3.

A cube built from 27 small cubes has 3 cubes along each edge. The cube root of the volume is the edge length.

Perfect cubes to know

1, 8, 27, 64, 125, 216, 343, 512, 729, 1,000. These are 1³ to 10³.

Negative numbers have cube roots

(−2) × (−2) × (−2) = −8, so ∛(−8) = −2. This is different from square roots: there is no real number that squares to a negative number, but every number has a cube root.

Do not mix them up: √64 = 8 but ∛64 = 4.

See three worked examples

∛125
  1. Which number used three times gives 125?
  2. 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

Answer: 5

∛(−64)
  1. 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.
  2. Three negatives multiply to a negative, so (−4) × (−4) × (−4) = −64.

Answer: −4

∛1000
  1. 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000.

Answer: 10
