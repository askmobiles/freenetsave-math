Order of operations with powers

When a question has several operations, everyone must do them in the same order to get the same answer.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

The agreed order is often remembered as BEDMAS:

  1. Brackets: work out anything inside brackets first.
  2. Exponents: powers such as 3² or 2³.
  3. Division and Multiplication: do these from left to right, whichever comes first.
  4. Addition and Subtraction: do these from left to right, whichever comes first.

Division does not always come before multiplication. They are equal partners, so you work from left to right. The same is true for addition and subtraction.

Why powers come before multiplying

A power is repeated multiplication: 3² means 3 × 3. In 2 × 3², the power belongs only to the 3, so you get 2 × 9 = 18. If you want the 2 included, you need brackets: (2 × 3)² = 6² = 36.

Watch for this: 2 + 3² is 2 + 9 = 11, but (2 + 3)² is 5² = 25. Brackets change the answer.

A good habit is to rewrite the whole line after each step, changing only one thing at a time. It is slower, but it stops most mistakes.

See three worked examples

3 + 2² × 5
  1. Exponent first: 2² = 4. The line becomes 3 + 4 × 5.
  2. Multiply next: 4 × 5 = 20. The line becomes 3 + 20.
  3. Add: 3 + 20 = 23.

Answer: 23

(3 + 2)² × 5
  1. Brackets first: 3 + 2 = 5. The line becomes 5² × 5.
  2. Exponent: 5² = 25. The line becomes 25 × 5.
  3. Multiply: 25 × 5 = 125.

Answer: 125

20 − 12 ÷ 2²
  1. Exponent first: 2² = 4. The line becomes 20 − 12 ÷ 4.
  2. Divide: 12 ÷ 4 = 3. The line becomes 20 − 3.
  3. Subtract: 20 − 3 = 17.

Answer: 17
