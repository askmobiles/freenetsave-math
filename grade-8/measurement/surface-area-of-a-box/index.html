Surface area of a box

Surface area is how much paper it takes to cover a box. Unfold the box and add up all six faces.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Unfold a box that is 6 cm long, 2 cm wide and 7 cm tall. You get its net: six rectangles in three matching pairs.

The steps

  1. Find the three different face areas: length × width, length × height and width × height.
  2. Add them.
  3. Double the total, because each face has a twin on the other side.

For the box above: 12 + 42 + 14 = 68, and 68 × 2 = 136 cm².

A cube has six faces that are all the same square, so its surface area is 6 × side × side.

No lid? An open box has only five faces. Count the bottom once, not twice.

See three worked examples

Find the surface area of a box 6 cm by 2 cm by 7 cm.
  1. Faces: 6 × 2 = 12, 6 × 7 = 42, 2 × 7 = 14.
  2. 12 + 42 + 14 = 68.
  3. 68 × 2.

Answer: 136 cm²

Find the surface area of a cube with sides of 5 cm.
  1. One face: 5 × 5 = 25.
  2. A cube has 6 faces: 6 × 25.

Answer: 150 cm²

Find the surface area of a box 10 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.
  1. Faces: 10 × 4 = 40, 10 × 3 = 30, 4 × 3 = 12.
  2. 40 + 30 + 12 = 82.
  3. 82 × 2.

Answer: 164 cm²
