Angle relationships

A few angle facts let you find almost any missing angle. Learn them once and spot which one fits.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

On a straight line and around a point

  • Angles on a straight line add to 180°.
  • Angles in a right angle add to 90°.
  • Angles around a point add to 360°.
  • Where two lines cross, the opposite angles are equal.

Parallel lines

When a line crosses two parallel lines:

  • Corresponding angles (in the same position at each crossing) are equal.
  • Alternate angles (on opposite sides, between the lines) are equal. They make a Z shape.
  • Co-interior angles (on the same side, between the lines) add to 180°. They make a C shape.

Name the fact you use. Writing "angles on a straight line" next to your working helps you check it.

See three worked examples

Two angles are on a straight line. One is 65°. Find the other.
  1. Angles on a straight line add to 180°.
  2. 180 − 65.

Answer: 115°

Two lines cross. One angle is 48°. Find the angle opposite it.
  1. Opposite angles are equal.

Answer: 48°

A line crosses two parallel lines. One co-interior angle is 105°. Find the other.
  1. Co-interior angles add to 180°.
  2. 180 − 105.

Answer: 75°
