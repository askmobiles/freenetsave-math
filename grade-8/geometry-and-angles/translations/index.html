Translations on a grid

A translation slides a shape without turning or flipping it. Every point moves the same distance in the same direction.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Triangle A is slid 4 right and 2 up to make triangle B. It keeps its size and faces the same way.

Moving the coordinates

  • Right: add to x. Left: subtract from x.
  • Up: add to y. Down: subtract from y.

4 right and 2 up is the rule (x, y) → (x + 4, y + 2). The corner (1, 1) goes to (5, 3).

x first, then y. Left and right change only x. Up and down change only y.

See three worked examples

Move (2, 3) 5 right and 1 down.
  1. Right 5: 2 + 5 = 7.
  2. Down 1: 3 − 1 = 2.

Answer: (7, 2)

Move (−1, 4) 3 left and 2 up.
  1. Left 3: −1 − 3 = −4.
  2. Up 2: 4 + 2 = 6.

Answer: (−4, 6)

A point moves from (3, 1) to (−2, 4). Describe the move.
  1. x: −2 − 3 = −5, so 5 left.
  2. y: 4 − 1 = 3, so 3 up.

Answer: 5 left and 3 up
