Equations with x on both sides

When x is on both sides, take the smaller x term away from both sides. Then it is a two-step equation.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Here x appears on both sides of the balance.

Take 3x from both sides. The balance stays level, and now x is on one side only.

Now solve as usual: take 2 from both sides (2x = 8), then divide by 2: x = 4.

Check

Left side: 5 × 4 + 2 = 22. Right side: 3 × 4 + 10 = 22. Both sides match.

Take away the smaller x term. That keeps the x term positive, which makes fewer sign mistakes.

See three worked examples

Solve 5x + 2 = 3x + 10.
  1. Take 3x from both sides: 2x + 2 = 10.
  2. Take 2 from both sides: 2x = 8.
  3. Divide by 2.

Answer: x = 4

Solve 2x + 9 = 5x − 6.
  1. The smaller x term is 2x. Take 2x from both sides: 9 = 3x − 6.
  2. Add 6 to both sides: 15 = 3x.
  3. Divide by 3.

Answer: x = 5

Solve 6x − 4 = 2x + 20.
  1. Take 2x from both sides: 4x − 4 = 20.
  2. Add 4: 4x = 24.
  3. Divide by 4.

Answer: x = 6
