Two-step equations

An equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side, do the same to the other, and it stays balanced.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

3x + 5 = 20 says that the two sides weigh the same.

To find x, undo what was done to it, in the reverse order. x was multiplied by 3, then 5 was added. So:

  1. Take 5 from both sides: 3x = 15.
  2. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.

Check your answer

Put it back in: 3 × 5 + 5 = 20. It works.

Undo the plus or minus first, then the times or divide. It is the opposite of the order you would use to work out the value.

See three worked examples

Solve 3x + 5 = 20.
  1. Take 5 from both sides: 3x = 15.
  2. Divide both sides by 3.

Answer: x = 5

Solve 4x − 7 = 21.
  1. Add 7 to both sides: 4x = 28.
  2. Divide both sides by 4.

Answer: x = 7

Solve x/3 + 4 = 10.
  1. Take 4 from both sides: x/3 = 6.
  2. Multiply both sides by 3.

Answer: x = 18
