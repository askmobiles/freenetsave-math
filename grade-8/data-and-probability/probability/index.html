Probability as a fraction

Probability is a fraction: the ways you can get what you want, over all the things that can happen.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

You pick one marble from this bag without looking.

There are 10 marbles and 3 are red, so the chance of red is 3/10. The chance of blue is 5/10 = 1/2.

The steps

  1. Count the ways to get what you want.
  2. Count everything that can happen.
  3. Write the first over the second and simplify.

A probability is always from 0 (impossible, like picking a yellow marble here) to 1 (certain, like picking a marble).

"Not" questions: the chance of not green is 1 − 2/10 = 8/10 = 4/5. You can also just count the 8 marbles that are not green.

See three worked examples

From the bag above, what is the chance of picking red?
  1. Red marbles: 3.
  2. All marbles: 10.

Answer: 3/10

You roll a normal die. What is the chance of an even number?
  1. Even numbers: 2, 4 and 6, so 3 ways.
  2. 6 possible results.
  3. 3/6 in simplest form.

Answer: 1/2

From the bag above, what is the chance of not picking green?
  1. Not green: 3 red + 5 blue = 8.
  2. All marbles: 10.
  3. 8/10 in simplest form.

Answer: 4/5
