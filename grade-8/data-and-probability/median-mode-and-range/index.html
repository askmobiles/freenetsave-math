Median, mode and range

Put the numbers in order first. Then the middle, the most common and the spread are easy to read.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Here are five numbers: 5, 4, 8, 8, 20. In order they are:

  • Median: the middle number once they are in order. Here it is 8.
  • Mode: the number that appears most often. Here it is 8, which appears twice.
  • Range: largest minus smallest. Here 20 − 4 = 16.

An even count

With 3, 7, 9, 15 there are two middle numbers, 7 and 9.

The median is halfway between them: (7 + 9) ÷ 2 = 8.

Order first. The most common mistake is taking the middle number before sorting.

See three worked examples

Find the median of 5, 4, 8, 8, 20.
  1. In order: 4, 5, 8, 8, 20.
  2. The middle (third) number.

Answer: 8

Find the range of 4, 10, 10, 17, 20.
  1. Largest is 20, smallest is 4.
  2. 20 − 4.

Answer: 16

Find the median of 15, 3, 9, 7.
  1. In order: 3, 7, 9, 15.
  2. Two middle numbers: 7 and 9.
  3. (7 + 9) ÷ 2.

Answer: 8
