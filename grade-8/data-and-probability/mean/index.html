The mean (average)

The mean shares everything out equally. Add all the numbers, then divide by how many there are.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test6 Challenge

Learn

Four friends scored 12, 3, 4 and 13 points. If they shared the points out equally, how many would each get?

Altogether they have 12 + 3 + 4 + 13 = 32 points. Shared between 4 people: 32 ÷ 4 = 8. That is the mean.

The steps

  1. Add all the numbers.
  2. Count how many numbers there are.
  3. Divide the total by the count.

The mean does not have to be one of the numbers, and it can be a decimal. The mean of 1, 2 and 4 is 7 ÷ 3 = 2.33…

Working backwards: mean × count = total. If 5 numbers have a mean of 6, they add up to 30.

See three worked examples

Find the mean of 12, 3, 4 and 13.
  1. Add: 12 + 3 + 4 + 13 = 32.
  2. There are 4 numbers.
  3. 32 ÷ 4.

Answer: 8

Find the mean of 13, 17, 11 and 19.
  1. Add: 13 + 17 + 11 + 19 = 60.
  2. There are 4 numbers.
  3. 60 ÷ 4.

Answer: 15

Find the mean of 2.5, 3.5, 4 and 6.
  1. Add: 2.5 + 3.5 + 4 + 6 = 16.
  2. There are 4 numbers.
  3. 16 ÷ 4.

Answer: 4
