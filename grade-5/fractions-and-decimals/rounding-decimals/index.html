Rounding decimals

To the nearest tenth. Grade 5 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. Tenths are the first digit after the point.
  2. Look at the next digit, the hundredths.
  3. 5 or more: round the tenths up. Otherwise leave it.
