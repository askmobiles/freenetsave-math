Area and perimeter

Rectangles. Grade 4 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. Area is the space inside: length times width.
  2. Perimeter is the distance around: add length and width, then double.
  3. Read the question to see which one it wants.

Read the worked examples next. Then try the practice questions, which show the steps after every answer.

Rectangle 25 m by 18 mPerimeter in metres?

Answer: 86
