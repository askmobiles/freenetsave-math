Estimating sums

Round first, then add. Grade 4 math.

1 Learn2 See3 Try4 Play5 Test

Learn the key ideas

  1. An estimate is a close answer that is quick to find.
  2. Round each number to the nearest hundred.
  3. Add the rounded numbers.

Read the worked examples next. Then try the practice questions, which show the steps after every answer.
