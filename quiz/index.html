How the quiz and test maker works

Every question is made fresh on your device from more than 500 skills that follow the Ontario math curriculum, so each new quiz is different. Easy picks friendlier numbers, Hard picks bigger numbers, negatives, decimals and fractions, and Mixed uses all three levels.

In a quiz, you check each answer as you go. In a test, you answer everything first and see your mark at the end, with the worked answer for every question and a breakdown by topic.

Teachers and tutors can print a test with an answer key, or copy a link so everyone gets the same questions. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
